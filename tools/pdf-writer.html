Text to PDF Converter — Fast, Free, and 100% Private

Converting your text into a clean, professional PDF has never been easier. The DocEasy Text to PDF Converter allows you to type directly into the editor or paste text from any source, format it with various fonts, colors, and alignments, and instantly save it as a standard PDF document.

Whether you're creating a quick report, saving notes for offline reading, or generating a formal letter, this tool runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to a server, ensuring complete privacy.

Key Features

  • Rich Text Editor: Bold, italicize, underline, change text color, and highlight important sections.
  • Built-in Translator: Instantly convert your document to Hindi, Chinese, Russian, or Spanish.
  • True A4 Print Preview: See exactly how your document will look on paper before printing.
  • Headers & Footers: Toggle on headers and footers to add page numbers, titles, or dates that print on every page.

How to Convert Text to PDF

  1. Enter Your Text: Type directly into the editor or paste your copied text.
  2. Format & Translate: Use the toolbar to bold words, change colors, or click Translate to convert to another language.
  3. Check the Preview: Click the "Preview" button to see exactly how your A4 pages will look.
  4. Save as PDF: Click the "Save PDF" button to open the print dialog and save your document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, the Text to PDF converter is completely free with no signup required.

Will my formatting be saved in the PDF?

Yes! When you save as PDF using the print dialog, all rich text formatting, colors, and fonts are preserved exactly as you see them in the editor.

Can I translate the text before saving as PDF?

Absolutely. Click the Translate button in the toolbar, select your language, and the tool will convert the text directly in the editor.
