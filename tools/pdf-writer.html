Key Features Explained

Here is a detailed breakdown of the tools available in the editor:

1. Rich Text Formatting

The editor includes a full suite of text formatting tools. You can bold your headings, italicize emphasis, underline key terms, and even apply custom text colors and highlights. This is crucial for making professional-looking documents. For example, if you are generating a report, you can highlight the key findings in yellow and change the font to a formal serif font.

Click the font dropdown to access a curated list of 14+ fonts. We have separated them into two categories:

This feature uses an internal API to translate your entire document into another language. It preserves the structure, numbers, and currency symbols (like ₹) while translating the text. It is highly accurate and saves you the trouble of opening a separate translation tab.

4. Header & Footer Toggles

For professional documents, headers and footers are essential. You can enable them via the toggle switches. Type your header (e.g., your company name) and it will automatically appear on the top of every A4 page in the preview and the final PDF. Similarly, you can add a footer (e.g., "Confidential" or page numbers).

5. Virtual Hindi Keyboard

If you do not have a Hindi keyboard installed on your computer, this tool is a lifesaver. Clicking the "Hindi Keyboard" button opens a virtual keyboard. You can click the consonants and vowels to type in Hindi without needing any system language packs.
