Text to PDF Converter — Fast, Free, and 100% Private

Converting your text into a clean, professional PDF has never been easier. The DocEasy Text to PDF Converter allows you to type directly into the editor or paste text from any source, format it with various fonts, colors, and alignments, and instantly save it as a standard PDF document.

Whether you're creating a quick report, saving notes for offline reading, or generating a formal letter, this tool runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to a server, ensuring complete privacy.

Key Features

  • Rich Text Editor: Bold, italicize, underline, change text color, and highlight important sections.
  • Custom Fonts: Choose from a wide variety of fonts, including standard English fonts and Devanagari (Hindi) fonts.
  • Headers & Footers: Toggle on headers and footers to add page numbers, titles, or dates that print on every page.
  • 100% Browser-Based: No files are uploaded. The conversion happens locally on your device.

How to Convert Text to PDF

  1. Enter Your Text: Type directly into the editor or paste your copied text.
  2. Format (Optional): Use the toolbar to bold words, change colors, adjust font sizes, or pick a custom font.
  3. Add Header/Footer (Optional): Turn on the toggles and type your header/footer text.
  4. Save as PDF: Click the "Save as PDF" button. In the print dialog that opens, select "Save as PDF" as your printer/destination, and click Save.
