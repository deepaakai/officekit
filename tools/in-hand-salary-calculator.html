Complete Guide to CTC, Take-Home Salary & Tax Regimes in India (2026)

Receiving a job offer with an impressive Cost to Company (CTC) figure is exciting, but the actual salary credited to your bank account each month (your "in-hand" or "take-home" salary) is often 15% to 30% lower. Understanding mandatory payroll deductions, statutory retirement contributions, and the differences between the New and Old tax regimes ensures you negotiate salary packages effectively.

1. Key Components of a Salary Structure

  • Basic Salary: The foundational core of your package, typically 40% to 50% of your CTC. Statutory benefits like Provident Fund (PF) and Gratuity are calculated as fixed percentages of Basic.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Granted to meet accommodation expenses. In the Old Tax Regime, HRA is partially or fully exempt under Section 10(13A).
  • Special Allowance: A taxable balancing allowance that fills the gap between basic, HRA, and overall CTC.
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF): A mandatory deduction of 12% of your Basic salary credited toward your retirement fund. Your employer contributes a matching 12% (counted inside your CTC).
  • Gratuity: Statutory terminal benefit calculated at 4.81% of your basic pay under the Payment of Gratuity Act, payable upon completing 5 years of service.

2. New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime: Which is Better?

Salaried employees can choose between two tax structures each financial year:

  • New Tax Regime: Offers significantly lower tax slab rates and an increased Standard Deduction of ₹75,000. Taxable income up to ₹7,00,000 incurs zero tax liability due to Section 87A rebate. However, traditional exemptions (HRA, 80C, 80D, home loan interest) are not permitted.
  • Old Tax Regime: Retains higher tax rates with a ₹50,000 standard deduction, but allows substantial deductions including Section 80C (up to ₹1.5L), Section 80D (health insurance up to ₹1L), Section 24(b) (home loan interest up to ₹2L), and actual HRA exemptions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why does a ₹15 Lakh CTC pay differently across different companies?

Employers structure CTC differently. Some companies include discretionary bonuses, company health insurance premiums, transport allowances, or higher employer retirement contributions inside the CTC figure. A package with higher Basic pay results in higher PF savings but slightly lower monthly in-hand cash.

How is HRA exemption calculated under Section 10(13A)?

In the Old Regime, HRA exemption equals the lowest of three amounts: (1) Actual HRA received, (2) Actual rent paid minus 10% of basic salary, or (3) 50% of basic salary for metro cities (40% for non-metros).
