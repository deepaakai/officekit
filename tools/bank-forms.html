How to Find the Correct Bank Form for KYC, Signature Update, Deposit, Withdrawal and Nomination

Every bank account holder needs a form at some point: to complete KYC, change a signature, update a mobile number, deposit cash, withdraw money, request a cheque book or add a nominee. Because every bank uses its own layout and version, the same request can need a different form at State Bank of India, HDFC Bank or Punjab National Bank. This page collects the most common forms in one place so that you can save time and avoid a wasted trip to the branch.

Common Forms and Bank-wise Forms

Some forms are driven by Reserve Bank of India rules and look very similar everywhere, for example KYC, Form 60, Form 15G, Form 15H, CKYC and nomination forms DA-1, DA-2 and DA-3. These are in the Common Forms tab. Other forms, such as an account opening form, a withdrawal slip or a locker application, are bank-specific. For these, use the Bank-wise Forms tab and pick your bank. Most banks accept their own format only, so a bank-wise form is the safer choice when you know your bank.

KYC Forms and Re-KYC

KYC (Know Your Customer) confirms your identity and address. You need it when opening an account and again at periodic intervals. A KYC form normally asks for your name, date of birth, address, PAN or Form 60, occupation, income range and a nominee. Attach self-attested copies of an officially valid document such as Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence. If your details have not changed, many banks accept a simple self-declaration for re-KYC. If they have changed, submit the KYC update form with fresh documents.

Signature Card and Signature Update

The signature card, also called the specimen signature card, is the signature record that the bank compares with your cheques and withdrawal slips. If your signature has changed because of age, injury or a legal name change, submit a signature update request with your ID proof and sign in front of a bank official. For joint accounts, the mode of operation, such as Either or Survivor, is recorded on the same card. If you operate the account with a thumb impression, a separate declaration is used.

Deposit Slip, Withdrawal Slip and Cheque Deposit Slip

A cash deposit slip, also called a pay-in slip, lists the account number, name, date, amount in figures and words and the denominations. A cash withdrawal slip is used to take cash from your own account and must carry the same signature as on your signature card. A cheque deposit slip is used when you deposit one or more cheques. For large cash transactions, banks ask for PAN or Form 60. When someone else deposits or withdraws money on your behalf, an authority letter or third-party declaration may be needed. Many banks also keep these slips free at the counter, and some now allow the same transactions through kiosks and apps.

Cheque Book Request, Stop Payment and Positive Pay

A cheque book requisition slip is usually available at the end of your last cheque book, in the passbook or at the branch. To stop payment of a cheque, submit a stop payment instruction with the cheque number, date, amount and payee. The bank may charge a fee. For high value cheques, banks use the Positive Pay System, where you confirm the cheque details before it is cleared. Lost cheque leaves should be reported at once in writing.

Nomination Forms: DA-1, DA-2 and DA-3

A nominee receives the money in your account after your death without a long legal process. The DA-1 form is used to nominate, DA-2 to cancel a nomination and DA-3 to vary it. You can also find forms for fixed deposits, safe deposit lockers, joint accounts and minor accounts. If you choose not to nominate, banks record this through a declaration, which is listed in the Nomination folder. The number of nominees and the rules can change, so check the current rules with your bank.

Aadhaar Seeding and NPCI Mapper

Aadhaar seeding means linking your Aadhaar number with your bank account so that subsidies, scholarships and other Direct Benefit Transfers reach the correct account. The consent form is used to seed Aadhaar and to map the account at NPCI. If you have more than one account, the most recently mapped account normally receives the benefit, so keep your preferred account updated.

Fixed Deposit, Loan, Locker and Closure Forms

For deposits, the folder contains FD and RD opening, premature closure, renewal instructions and the tax-related Form 15G and Form 15H. For loans, you will find personal, home, vehicle, education, gold, MSME, Mudra and Kisan Credit Card applications, along with closure and NOC requests. Locker forms cover application, nomination and surrender. Account closure, dormant account reactivation, deceased claim settlement and grievance forms are available in the closure folder.

Debit Card and ATM Card Forms

If your debit card is lost or stolen, block it first through the bank helpline, mobile app or net banking, then submit the block and reissue request at the branch. Banks may ask for a signed declaration and sometimes a copy of the FIR. For a damaged or expired card, use the replacement request. If you forget your PIN, use the PIN reset or re-PIN request. You can also change daily limits and switch domestic, international or contactless usage on or off with the limit and usage form. A new card normally reaches your registered address, so keep your address and mobile number updated.

Credit Card Lost, Replacement and Dispute Forms

For a lost credit card, report the loss immediately and ask the bank to block the card and issue a replacement. Keep the complaint reference number safely. The credit card section also has forms for PIN set or reset, limit increase, add-on cards, EMI conversion, balance transfer, address update, auto-debit and closure. If an unknown transaction appears on your statement, file the dispute or chargeback form at once and attach the statement page.

Failed Transactions, Fraud and Complaints

When money is debited from your account but the ATM does not dispense cash, or a UPI or IMPS payment fails, use the failed transaction complaint form and attach the receipt or reference number. For an unauthorised transaction, report it to the bank as early as possible, because the time of reporting can affect your liability. For a wrong transfer, use the reversal request. If the bank does not solve your complaint within the time limit, escalate to the nodal officer and later to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.

Passbook, Statement and Account Service Forms

Lost passbook, passbook printing, certified statements for visa or loan files, home branch transfer, adding or removing a joint holder, minor to major conversion, account type conversion, freeze or de-freeze requests and lien requests are common branch visits. Having these forms printed in advance saves a long wait at the counter.

Tips to Avoid Rejection

  • Use the latest version of the form and confirm it with your bank.
  • Write in block letters with a black or blue pen and avoid overwriting.
  • Make sure your signature matches the signature on record.
  • Attach self-attested copies and carry the originals for verification.
  • Keep an acknowledgement of every request that you submit.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or card numbers while filling a form.

Beware of Fraud

Bank forms are free. A bank will never ask you to pay a third party for a form or to share your OTP, CVV or PIN. Submit forms only at the branch, through the bank's official website or app, or through an authorised representative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I download bank KYC form PDF?

Open the Common Forms tab for a standard KYC form or choose your bank in Bank-wise Forms and open the KYC folder. Always confirm the latest version on your bank's website.

How do I update my signature card in a bank?

Download the Signature Change or Update Request form, sign it in the presence of a bank official with your ID proof and submit it at your home branch.

Is a withdrawal slip or deposit slip available in PDF?

Yes. The Deposit and Withdrawal Slips folder has cash deposit, cash withdrawal and cheque deposit slips. Many banks also keep them free at the branch counter.

What is the nomination form for a bank account?

Nomination forms such as DA-1, DA-2 and DA-3 are used to add, cancel or change a nominee. Forms for accounts without a nominee are also listed.

What is Aadhaar seeding in a bank account?

Aadhaar seeding links your Aadhaar number with your bank account so that government benefits can be received through DBT. Use the Aadhaar Seeding consent form in the KYC folder.

Is DocEasy an official bank website?

No. DocEasy is a private information website and is not affiliated with any bank or with the Reserve Bank of India.

What should I do if my debit card is lost or stolen?

Block the card at once through the bank helpline, app or net banking, then submit the Lost Debit Card Block and Reissue form at your branch. Some banks ask for a lost card declaration or an FIR copy.

How do I report a failed ATM or UPI transaction?

Download the ATM Cash Not Dispensed or UPI Failed Transaction complaint form, attach your transaction details and submit it to your bank. Report quickly, because banks follow time limits for reversal.
