What is a JPG to PDF Converter?

A JPG to PDF converter is a tool that combines one or more image files — typically JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WEBP — into a single Portable Document Format (PDF) file. Instead of sending 15 separate photos to your bank, your college, or a client, you send one clean PDF that opens correctly on any device.

Common alternative names for this tool include image to PDF converter, photo to PDF maker, picture to PDF, scan to PDF app, and document scanner online. All describe the same basic job: turn image files into a professional, print-ready PDF.

How to Convert JPG to PDF — Step by Step

  1. Add your JPG images: Click Camera Capture for direct scan, or Gallery / Files for existing photos. Drag-and-drop works too. JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP accepted — up to 30 pages.
  2. Crop (optional): Tap any thumbnail to open the editor. Drag 4 corner handles to fix perspective. Or skip — the full image is preserved by default.
  3. Reorder, duplicate, add blank pages: Drag pages on the grid. Insert blank A4 pages. Duplicate signature blocks.
  4. Export PDF: Choose page size (A4 / A3 / Letter / Auto), orientation, margin, quality. Optionally watch ad to remove watermark. Click Create & Save PDF.

White Background & Page Size

By default, DocEasy places each image on a clean A4 page with white background. If your image doesn't match A4's aspect ratio, the extra space stays white — just like a professionally scanned document. Adjust the margin between 0–30 mm to control the white border around your image.

Choose Auto (Match Image) page size if you want the PDF page to match the image exactly — no white background, edge-to-edge content. Perfect for photos, screenshots, and images already sized to A4.

Privacy, Safety & How It Works

Every step happens in your browser — no data transmitted:

  1. Loading: Files read using FileReader and createImageBitmap. Stay in RAM only.
  2. Editing: Cropping, perspective, filters run on <canvas>.
  3. PDF generation: PDF-lib library assembles pages. Fully client-side.
  4. Download: A Blob URL generated locally. No network carries your image data.

Safe for Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements, medical reports, contracts, and NDAs.

Pro Tips for the Best Scans

  • Dark matte background: Place document on black or grey surface for sharper crop accuracy.
  • Hold phone parallel: Perpendicular photos reduce perspective distortion.
  • Even, diffused light: Avoid direct sunlight and hard shadows.
  • Fill the frame: Document should occupy most of the photo.
  • One page per shot: Individual photos for cleaner output.
  • Use "Light Text" filter for faded ink: Ideal for old certificates.
