How to Use JPG to PDF & Document Scanner

Follow these simple steps to compile your images into a professional, searchable PDF document directly in your browser.

Interactive browser-based scanner interface with perspective crop and filters.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  • Step 1: Upload or Capture - Use your device camera or pick files from your gallery.
  • Step 2: Perspective Correction - Tap any page to open the editor. Drag the 4 corner handles to the edges of the paper.
  • Step 3: Enhance - Apply filters like 'Grayscale' or 'Auto Color' to make text legible.
  • Step 4: Reorder - Drag and drop thumbnails to organize your pages.
  • Step 5: Export - Choose page size, name your file, and download.
