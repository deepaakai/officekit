Turn Photos Into a Polished PDF — With a Built-In Document Editor

Phone photos of paperwork rarely look print-ready straight out of the camera: the page is skewed, the lighting is uneven, and the edges of the desk sneak into the shot. OfficeKit's JPG to PDF tool adds a scan-style editor on top of the usual image-to-PDF conversion, so every page can be squared up, brightened and cleaned before it's compiled — all inside your browser, with nothing uploaded to a server.

What you can do before exporting

  • Crop and straighten: auto-detect the document edges or drag the four corner handles yourself to correct perspective.
  • Rotate: fix sideways or upside-down photos in one tap.
  • Apply a filter: Auto-color, Light Text, Grayscale, Whiteboard or Enhance — with an option to apply the same look to every page.
  • Fine-tune: adjust brightness, contrast and saturation with live sliders.
  • Reorder pages: drag thumbnails into the order you want them to appear in the final PDF.

FAQ

Is anything uploaded to a server?
No — every crop, filter and the final PDF are generated locally in your browser using the Canvas API and pdf-lib.

Can I mix JPG, PNG and WEBP files in one PDF?
Yes, they can all be combined into a single document.
