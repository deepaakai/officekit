How to Use JPG to PDF & Document Scanner

Complete guide on scanning and compiling images into high-quality PDFs securely in your browser.

Interactive browser-based scanner interface with perspective crop and filters.

Welcome to the ultimate client-side Document Scanner and JPG to PDF conversion tool. Whether you are dealing with multi-page handwritten notes, important invoices, identity papers, certificates, or formal receipts, this tool lets you process everything directly within your web browser without uploading a single byte to any remote server. Your files remain 100% private, secure, and under your absolute control.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Upload or Capture: Click "Camera Capture" to take live photos using your smartphone camera, or select "Gallery / Files" to pick multiple images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) at once. You can also drag and drop image files directly into the drop zone.
  2. Crop and Perspective Correction: Tap any thumbnail page to open the live editor. Drag the four corner handles to align the boundaries with your document edges. This removes unwanted background space and corrects awkward camera angles instantly.
  3. Apply Filters & Adjustments: Switch to the Filter tab to enhance readability. Choose presets like Auto Color, Light Text, Grayscale, or Whiteboard. You can also apply a filter across all loaded pages using "Apply Filter to All Pages". Fine-tune brightness, contrast, and saturation with the sliders.
  4. Organize & Reorder: Easily reorder pages by dragging and dropping them into your preferred sequence, or use the move and duplicate tools. You can also add blank pages if needed.
  5. Export to PDF: Configure page size (A4, A3, Letter, or Auto), adjust margins, select image compression quality, and click "Create & Save PDF". Your document compiles instantly and downloads straight to your local device.

Why Choose Client-Side Processing?

Traditional online tools require you to upload personal documents over the internet, posing severe privacy risks. Our advanced browser utility uses modern Web APIs, client-side canvas transformations, and script engines to compile everything locally. There are no limits, no subscription fees, no intrusive watermarks, and no waiting queues.
