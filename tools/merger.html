Universal File Merger — PDF, Word, Excel, PPT Combined (2026)

DocEasy's Universal File Merger is a single tool for combining any type of document — PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and PowerPoint (.pptx). Choose your file type, upload multiple files, reorder them by dragging, and download the merged result directly. Everything runs in your browser — nothing uploads to any server, and your documents stay completely private.

Direct PPT Merging Without ZIP

Unlike other online tools that force you into a ZIP archive, DocEasy combines presentation files directly inside the browser into a single, native .pptx presentation. It automatically registers all slides, re-maps relationship IDs, and preserves embedded slide media so you can open your combined presentation directly in Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Keynote without errors. You also get an instant option to download the entire deck as a single, beautifully formatted PDF.

How to Use — Step by Step

  1. Step 1 — Choose file type: Click the toggle for PDF, Word, Excel, or PPT.
  2. Step 2 — Upload files: Drag and drop or click to browse. At least two files are required.
  3. Step 3 — Reorder (optional): Drag files or use ▲▼ arrows to change merge order.
  4. Step 4 — Configure options: Customize settings like bookmarks, page breaks, or PDF generation.
  5. Step 5 — Click "Merge Files": Processing happens locally — takes seconds.
  6. Step 6 — Download: Save your single combined file directly to your computer.

Privacy and Security

  • No uploads: Files stay on your device
  • No server storage: Nothing is saved anywhere except your own computer
