How the AI Photo Beautifier Works

This tool combines fast pixel-level algorithms (skin smoothing, sharpening, clarity, color balance) with an optional AI super-resolution mode powered by Real-ESRGAN. Everything runs inside your browser — your photos never leave your device.

What Each Slider Does

  • Skin Smoothing: Applies edge-preserving blur only to skin-toned pixels. Eyelashes, eyes, and lips stay sharp.
  • Sharpness: Unsharp mask that makes eyes, hair, and textures pop — gives that "HD" look.
  • Clarity: Local contrast enhancement. Adds micro-contrast to make details stand out.
  • Brightness / Contrast: Standard tonal adjustment.
  • Saturation / Warmth: Color science for natural, healthy-looking skin tones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All processing runs locally in your browser. Verify with Developer Tools → Network tab.
