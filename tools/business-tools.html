How accurate is the GST calculator?

The GST calculator uses the official GST slabs and formulas as prescribed by the CBIC (Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs). It supports all current GST rates: 0%, 0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. For intra-state transactions, GST splits equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). For inter-state transactions, IGST (Integrated GST) applies at the full rate. The calculator handles both inclusive and exclusive GST, and correctly computes the tax amount, taxable value, and total. The math is precise to 2 decimal places — same as most commercial accounting software.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

Under India's GST regime, taxes are split based on whether the transaction is intra-state or inter-state. CGST (Central GST): The central government's share of tax on intra-state sales. SGST (State GST): The state government's share on intra-state sales. Together, CGST + SGST equal the total GST rate (e.g., 9% + 9% = 18%). IGST (Integrated GST): Applies to inter-state sales (between different states). IGST is collected by the central government and later distributed to the destination state. For example, a sale within Maharashtra attracts CGST + SGST, while a sale from Maharashtra to Karnataka attracts IGST. DocEasy's GST calculator lets you pick intra-state or inter-state and auto-splits the tax correctly.

What is reverse GST calculation and when do I need it?

Reverse GST calculation extracts the base price and tax amount from a total that already includes GST. For example, if a customer pays ₹1,180 for a product and you know the GST rate is 18%, the reverse calculation tells you that the base price is ₹1,000 and GST is ₹180. This is useful when: (1) You're working backwards from a customer's budget ("I have ₹10,000 total — what's the base amount at 18% GST?"); (2) You receive an invoice that shows only the total and you need to verify the tax; (3) You're booking expenses where the total is known but the GST split needs to be recorded separately. DocEasy's Reverse GST Calculator handles this in one click.

What is stamp duty and why do I need to calculate it?

Stamp duty is a tax levied by state governments on legal documents like agreements, property transfers, affidavits, and contracts. The rate varies by state and document type — for example, Maharashtra charges 5% + ₹5,000 for property transfer, while Delhi charges 4–6% depending on the buyer's gender. Stamp duty must be paid before or at the time of document execution, otherwise the document becomes inadmissible as evidence in court. Our upcoming Stamp Duty Calculator will help you estimate the correct duty for common document types across Indian states. This is especially useful for rental agreements, sale deeds, gift deeds, and partnership contracts.
