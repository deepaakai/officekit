What are DocEasy Business Tools?

DocEasy Business Tools are a growing collection of free, browser-based utilities for Indian small businesses, freelancers, shopkeepers, and eMitra/CSC operators. They include a GST calculator (with CGST, SGST, IGST splits), reverse GST calculator, invoice generator, stamp duty estimator, quotation maker, purchase order generator, HS code finder, and business loan EMI calculator. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no uploads, no signup, no subscription fees.

Are these business tools really free?

Yes — every tool is 100% free, forever, with no strings attached. There's no signup, no email, no credit card, no trial period, and no premium tier.

Is my business data safe?

Completely safe. Every business tool runs entirely client-side in your browser — no data is ever uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.
