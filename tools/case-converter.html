Understanding Different Types of Text Cases

English has specific rules for how text should look. Here is a breakdown of the different cases you can create with this text capitalization tool:

Sentence Case

This is the standard way we write. The first letter of the first word is capitalized, and the rest is lowercase, unless there is a proper noun.
Example: The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

Title Case

Used for book titles, movie names, and article headings. Major words are capitalized. Small words like "and", "of", "the" are usually lowercase unless they are at the beginning.
Example: The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over the Lazy Dog.

Uppercase

Every single letter is a capital letter. Used for acronyms (like NASA, USA) or for extreme emphasis.
Example: THE QUICK BROWN FOX JUMPS OVER THE LAZY DOG.

Lowercase

Every single letter is a small letter. Used in coding, web addresses, and some modern brand logos.
Example: the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I change text from lowercase to uppercase?

Paste your lowercase text into the input box and click the "UPPERCASE" button. All the letters will instantly turn into capital letters.

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence Case?

In Sentence case, only the first letter of the sentence is capitalized (like a normal sentence). In Title Case, the first letter of every major word is capitalized (like a headline or a book title).

Does the tool remove extra spaces?

Our current tool focuses only on changing the letter case. It will not remove extra spaces between words. However, you can easily do that manually.

Can I use this to change text for my Excel sheet?

Yes. You can convert the text here, copy it, and paste it directly into your Excel cells or Google Sheets.

What is alternating case used for?

Alternating case (aLtErNaTiNg cAsE) is mostly used for fun, memes, or to give a sarcastic tone to a message on social media. It doesn't have a formal grammar use.
