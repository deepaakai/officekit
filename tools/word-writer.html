Word Writer & Translator — Free Online Word Processor

Word Writer & Translator is DocEasy's free, browser-based word processor built for global users. It allows you to create, format, translate, and export documents without installing heavy software like Microsoft Word or Google Docs. Whether you're typing a simple English letter, a Hindi assignment using Hinglish, or translating an entire document into Chinese or Russian, this tool adapts to your workflow. It runs entirely locally in your browser, ensuring 100% privacy, and offers a genuine A4 print preview so you never waste paper.

1. Built-in Document Translator

Unlike other word processors that require you to copy-paste text into a separate translation tool, Word Writer has the translator built right into the toolbar. By clicking the blue "Translate" button, you can translate your entire document instantly.

The translator uses a highly accurate internal Google Translate API. It carefully preserves numbers, currencies (₹), and proper nouns (like names and addresses) while converting the surrounding text into your target language. Supported languages include:

  • Hindi (हिन्दी): Perfect for official applications, leave letters, and academic assignments.
  • Chinese (中文), Russian (Русский), Arabic (العربية): For international communication, with universal fonts ensuring no broken characters.
  • Spanish, French, German, and English: For global business and travel.

Tip: After translating, you can manually click on any word to edit it if you want to fine-tune the translation.

2. True A4 Print Preview (No More Wasted Paper)

Have you ever printed a document online, only to find that the text has run off the page or created a huge empty gap? Word Writer solves this with its True A4 Print Preview.

When you click the "Preview" button, the tool renders your text exactly as it will appear on an A4 sheet of paper. If your document is long, it intelligently splits the content into Page 1, Page 2, Page 3, etc., complete with accurate page breaks. You can review the layout, check your margins, and ensure your headers and footers are in place before clicking "Print / Save PDF."

3. Smart Auto-Naming & File Counter

We know how frustrating it is to download multiple documents named "Untitled" and then have to rename them manually. Word Writer features a smart file-naming system.

When you translate a document, the title automatically updates to reflect the new language. When you save the file (as PDF or DOCX), the tool appends a unique counter to the filename (e.g., Word Translator - Chinese - 1). After saving, the counter automatically increments to - 2 for your next download. This ensures that you never accidentally overwrite a previous file, and you save time on manual renaming.

4. International Language & Font Support

Word Writer is built for global use. By integrating Noto Sans and Noto Serif (Google's universal fonts), we ensure that every character from every major script (Latin, Cyrillic, Devanagari, Arabic, Chinese) renders flawlessly.

For Hindi typing, you can choose from the modern Noto Sans Devanagari or the official Government of India font, Mangal. When you export your document, the text remains crisp and readable across all devices and PDF viewers.

5. Three Typing Modes (English, Hinglish, Hindi)

We understand the diverse typing habits of users. The toolbar offers three distinct modes:

  • English Mode (Default): Standard left-to-right typing for international users. Spell-check is enabled.
  • Hinglish Mode: Type Hindi words using English letters. For example, typing "namaste" and pressing Space will automatically convert it to "नमस्ते". A smart suggestion bar helps you pick the right word.
  • Hindi Mode: Opens a fully categorized Virtual Devanagari Keyboard. You can click Consonants, Vowels, and Matras to form perfect Hindi words without needing a physical Hindi keyboard.

6. Rich Header & Footer

For professional documents, headers and footers are essential. You can toggle them on using the switches below the editor. Unlike other tools, our headers and footers are fully rich-text enabled. You can highlight the text inside them and use the toolbar to make it Bold, Italic, or change the font size. They will print cleanly on every A4 page in the PDF.

Step-by-Step: How to Use Word Writer

  1. Start with a Template or Blank Page: Choose from Formal Letter, Leave Application, Resume, or Notice to save time.
  2. Type Your Content: Use English, Hinglish, or Hindi mode. Use the toolbar for bold, italics, lists, and fonts.
  3. Translate (Optional): Click "Translate" in the toolbar, select your target language (e.g., Chinese or Russian), and click "Translate Now". The title will update automatically.
  4. Add Header/Footer (Optional): Toggle the header/footer switches and type your institution's name. You can bold the text directly.
  5. Check the Preview: Click the blue "Preview" button to see your A4 pages. Ensure the page breaks look correct.
  6. Export and Save: Click Print / Save PDF to get a perfect PDF, or Save Word (.docx) to download an editable Word file. The file will be named with your language and an incremented counter.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does the built-in translator cost extra?

No. The translation feature is 100% free and uses Google Translate's internal API. You can translate your entire document into multiple languages without paying a penny.

Will the PDF look exactly like the Print Preview?

Yes. The Print Preview is designed to match the final PDF output exactly. When you click "Print / Save PDF" from the preview screen, your browser's print dialog will open. Ensure the margins are set to "Default" or "Minimum" for the best result.

How does the Auto-Naming feature prevent file overwrites?

Every time you save a file, the tool increments a counter stored in your browser's local storage. The next time you save, the filename changes from, for example, "Word Translator - Hindi - 1" to "Word Translator - Hindi - 2". This guarantees that your browser will never ask you to "Replace" an existing file.

Can I edit the text after translating it?

Absolutely. The translation happens directly inside the editor. Once translated, you can click anywhere in the text and type normally to correct any words or format the text.

Does it support international fonts like Arabic or Russian?

Yes. By selecting the "Noto Sans" or "Noto Serif" universal fonts from the font picker, your text will render perfectly in Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, and over 100 other languages.

Privacy & Security

Word Writer runs entirely in your browser (client-side). Your document text, headers, and footers are never sent to our servers for storage. The only external requests are for loading the fonts and making the translation API call when you explicitly click the "Translate" button. For maximum privacy, you can confidently use Word Writer for confidential letters and applications.
