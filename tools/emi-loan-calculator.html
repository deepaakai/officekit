Complete Guide to Bank Reducing Balance EMI & Private Loan Interest Ledger

Financial clarity is essential when entering debt obligations. Whether securing an institutional home mortgage from registered commercial banks (reducing balance EMI) or executing a personal peer-to-peer loan with monthly simple interest (रुपये सैकड़ा ब्याज / सूद), keeping an authentic, auditable paper trail prevents costly financial disputes.

Dual-Copy Execution System (दोन प्रतियों में कागजी समझौता)

In peer-to-peer and private lending, verbal or unrecorded promises often lead to misunderstandings. DocEasy implements an authentic Dual-Copy Promissory Ledger workflow:

  1. Calculate the Loan Terms: Enter the sanctioned amount, interest rate (% p.a. for reducing bank loans or % per month for private credit), and tenure.
  2. Provide Party & Photo Details: Enter the names of both the Lender (देनदार) and Borrower (कर्जदार). Upload passport photos using the photo buttons, or leave them empty to glue physical passport photos later.
  3. Print Exactly Two Copies: Click Download PDF and print two identical copies on paper.
  4. Mutual Signatures & Cross-Attestation: Both parties sign across their respective photos (Sign Across Photo) and at the bottom on both physical sheets.
  5. Manual Offline Repayment Logging: Each month when interest or principal cash is exchanged, record the payment date, amount received, and receiver's signature directly on both papers with a pen.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I print two copies of this agreement for physical record-keeping? (दो कॉपी कैसे काम करती हैं?)

Yes, absolutely. This is the recommended approach for private loans. Print two identical PDF copies: one original for the lender and one counterpart for the borrower. Whenever an installment is paid, record the date, amount, and signature on both sheets simultaneously for 100% mutual transparency.

What does ₹2 सैकड़ा (2 Rupees Saikda) mean in private money lending?

In India, '2 रुपये सैकड़ा' means 2% simple interest per month (₹2 per ₹100 per month). On an annual basis, this equals a 24% simple interest rate per year.

Does this document work internationally outside India?

Yes. Simply click $ USD. All metrics, currency symbols, and legal undertaking terms automatically adapt to international promissory note standards recognized globally.

Why is there a cross-signature instruction on the photo?

Signing across the photograph (half signature on the paper, half across the photo) prevents anyone from tampering with or replacing the photograph, ensuring the document cannot be forged.
