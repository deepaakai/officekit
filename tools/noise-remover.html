Remove Background Noise from Audio & Video — Free Online (2026)

Recording a podcast, lecture, interview, or video? Background noise from fans, AC units, traffic, or electrical hum can ruin an otherwise great recording. DocEasy's Noise Remover cleans up your audio entirely inside your browser — no upload, no signup, no limits, and no watermarks. Supports both audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A) and video files (MP4, WEBM).

What This Tool Removes

  • Low-frequency rumble — AC units, traffic, table vibrations, mic handling.
  • Electrical hum — 50 Hz and 60 Hz hum from power lines.
  • High-frequency hiss — tape hiss, cheap mic noise, digital artifacts.
  • Steady background drone — fans, refrigerators, distant traffic.

How to Use — Audio Files

  1. Upload: Click or drag-drop MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, or WEBM.
  2. Choose strength: Light, Medium, Strong, or Extreme.
  3. Click "Remove Noise": Processing takes a few seconds.
  4. Compare: Play original and processed versions.
  5. Download: Save cleaned audio as WAV.

How to Use — Video Files

  1. Upload: Click or drag-drop MP4, WEBM, or MOV.
  2. Preview video: Video plays in the preview player.
  3. Choose strength and click "Remove Noise".
  4. Download Clean Audio: Save the cleaned audio track as WAV.
  5. Re-mux (optional): Use CapCut or FFmpeg to attach the clean audio back to the video.

Which Strength to Choose

  • Light: Subtle noise reduction — best when audio quality is already decent.
  • Medium: Balanced — the recommended default for most recordings.
  • Strong: Aggressive — useful for very noisy recordings or old tapes.
  • Extreme: Maximum filtering — may slightly affect voice quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this noise remover free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no email, no daily limit. Files are processed entirely in your browser.

Does it work with video files?

Yes. Upload MP4, WEBM, or MOV — the tool extracts the audio track, cleans it, and gives you a cleaned WAV file you can attach back to your video.

Are my files uploaded to any server?

Never. All processing uses the Web Audio API inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, WEBM, MP4, and any format your browser can decode. Output is always WAV for maximum compatibility.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the page loads once, disconnect from the internet and the tool still works.

Can it remove background music from a video?

Not currently. Music is treated as part of the audio signal. Vocal isolation would require AI-based source separation.

How do I attach the clean audio back to the video?

Use CapCut (free) — import video, delete original audio, add clean WAV file. Or use FFmpeg command line.

Will it affect my voice quality?

Medium and Light modes preserve voice quality. Strong and Extreme may cause slight artifacts — use only when necessary.
