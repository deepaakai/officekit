Why Should You Mask Your Aadhaar Number?

Your Aadhaar card is one of the most important documents in India. If it falls into the wrong hands, it can be used for identity theft, fake SIM cards, or fraudulent loans. To prevent this, the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) recommends that you always mask the first 8 digits of your Aadhaar number when sharing a photocopy.

By showing only the last 4 digits (e.g., XXXX XXXX 1234), the person can verify your identity, but they cannot misuse your full Aadhaar number.

When Should You Mask Your Data?

  • Rent Agreements: When sending your ID proof to a landlord.
  • Job Applications: When HR asks for your Aadhaar or PAN for background checks.
  • Social Media: Never post a screenshot of your Aadhaar card online without masking it.
  • Delivery Apps: Some apps ask for ID proof for age verification or high-value deliveries.
