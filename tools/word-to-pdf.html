Why Should You Convert DOCX to PDF?

Word documents are editable, which is great when you are writing, but it also means the file can shift its layout the moment it is opened on a different computer. A PDF locks every line, image, and table in place. That makes it the safest format for anything that needs to look identical everywhere — job applications, legal contracts, academic papers, invoices, and official forms.

Another reason is security. PDFs can be protected, and they discourage casual editing. When you send a Word file, the receiver can easily change its contents. When you send a PDF, your document stays exactly as you created it. Furthermore, PDFs are universally accepted by all government portals, job application systems (like Naukri or LinkedIn), and educational institutions.

Limitations to Keep in Mind

While our tool is highly capable, it is important to understand its limitations:

  • Complex Formatting: If your Word document contains complex tables, multi-column layouts, or floating images, the output PDF might require minor formatting tweaks.
  • Non-English Characters: This tool uses a standard English PDF font (Helvetica). If your document contains Hindi, Tamil, Arabic, Chinese, or Emoji, those characters will appear as "?". For Hindi documents, we recommend using a Unicode-capable desktop software.
  • Images: The current version focuses on extracting text. For image-heavy documents, consider our JPG to PDF tool instead.
