Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about DocEasy's tools for eMitra, CSC, Jan Seva Kendra, and cyber café operators.

What are DocEasy eMitra & CSC Tools?

DocEasy's eMitra & CSC Tools are a specialized collection of free, browser-based utilities designed for digital service center operators across India — including eMitra (Rajasthan), CSC (Common Service Centers under MeitY), Jan Seva Kendra, e-District portals, and cyber cafés. These tools automate cropping ID cards to exact 8.5 × 5.5 cm size, resizing photos for online applications, compressing PDFs, and scanning documents. Every tool runs locally in your browser.

Are these tools really free for commercial use?

Yes — every tool is 100% free forever with no restrictions on commercial use. Operators can process customer documents without registration or license fees.

Is my customer's Aadhaar, PAN, or ID data safe?

Completely safe. Every tool processes files in local memory via JavaScript and Canvas. Zero bytes are uploaded to our servers.

How do I crop a customer's Aadhaar card to the correct size?

Open the ID Card Dual-Side Cropper tool, load the PDF or scan, align the green (Front) and blue (Back) crop boxes, and click "Print Ultra HD" or "Save HD Images".

Does the tool work offline?

Yes. Once the page is loaded, all processing runs client-side without needing an active internet connection.
