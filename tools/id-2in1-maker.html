What is a 2 in 1 ID Maker?

A 2 in 1 ID Maker is a browser-based tool that takes two separate photos of an ID card — the front side and the back side — and merges them onto a single A4-size page. The final output is a print-ready document where both sides of your card appear side by side (or stacked vertically) in their actual physical size of 85.6 mm × 54 mm. You can then print this on any A4 paper using any home printer, cyber cafe printer, or office printer.

This is useful because almost every Indian government form, bank account application, school admission form, exam registration, and KYC process requires you to attach a photocopy of both sides of your ID card. Traditionally, people photocopy the front and back separately, which wastes two pages of paper. With a 2-in-1 ID maker, you save paper, save time, and produce a cleaner, more professional document.

Why AI Background Removal + Auto-Crop Matters

When you photograph an ID card on a table, the photo usually includes the table surface, shadows, other documents, your hand, or the phone casting a shadow. Traditional cropping tools force you to manually select the card, but if the background has other rectangular objects, the auto-detect can pick the wrong edges. Our new AI mode fixes this by running a neural network inside your browser that separates the card from whatever is behind it. The tool then detects the card's bounding box and auto-crops with a small ~0.5 cm margin on all four sides, so the card sits neatly in the final layout. You can still fine-tune manually with the Adjust Corners editor at any time.

How to Use the 2 in 1 ID Maker — Step by Step

  1. Upload the Front Side: Click the left slot or drag-and-drop a photo. AI removes the background and auto-crops the card with a small margin.
  2. Rotate If Needed: Use the ↺ or ↻ buttons to rotate 90° left or right. Two clicks give 180°.
  3. Manual Adjust (optional): If auto-crop misses, tap "Adjust Corners" and drag the four blue circles to the exact card corners. Use the zoom loupe for precision.
  4. Upload the Back Side: Repeat the same process for the back side.
  5. Apply Scan Filters: Choose from Original, Auto, Sepia, Magic Color, Grayscale, B&W Doc, or Whiteboard.
  6. Choose Layout & Size: Horizontal Side-by-Side on A4 Portrait (top-aligned), or Vertical Stacked on A4 Landscape (centered). Choose Actual Size (85.6 × 54 mm) or Large (100 × 63 mm).
  7. Download: Click "Download PDF" for a 300 DPI print-ready A4 PDF, or "Download JPG" for a share-ready image.

Best Results — Photography Tips

  • Use a dark matte surface: Place the card on a dark table, black cloth, or dark matte paper for maximum contrast.
  • Use natural daylight: Photograph near a window. Avoid flash and yellow indoor lights.
  • Keep the card flat: Don't hold it in your hand — the curves create distortion.
  • Hold the camera parallel: Keep the phone directly above the card.
  • Fill the frame: Get close while keeping all four corners visible.

Printing Tips for Perfect Output

  • Use A4 paper: Standard 210 × 297 mm.
  • Print at "Actual Size" or "100% Scale": Never "Fit to Page".
  • Use a laser printer for sharper text.
  • 80-100 GSM paper is ideal for KYC documentation.

Technical Details

  • AI background removal: @imgly/background-removal (WebAssembly in browser).
  • Auto-crop: Bounding box of non-transparent pixels + 6% margin.
  • Perspective correction: Homography-based warp with bilinear interpolation.
  • PDF generation: pdf-lib 1.17.1, generating a 300 DPI A4 page.
  • Output: A4 Portrait for Side-by-Side, A4 Landscape for Stacked.
  • Card size: CR80 standard 85.6 × 54 mm or 100 × 63 mm.
