2 in 1 ID Maker — Merge Aadhaar, PAN Front and Back on One Page

Every bank, government portal, exam authority, and office asks for a copy of your ID card on a single page. But when you photograph the front and back of your Aadhaar, PAN, or Voter ID, you end up with two separate images that take two pages to print. DocEasy's 2 in 1 ID Maker solves this — it rotates, auto-detects the card, crops both sides, merges them into one clean A4 page, and exports a print-ready PDF in seconds, all inside your browser.

How to Use

  1. Upload Front Side: Click the left slot and choose your front-side photo.
  2. Rotate if Needed: Use the ↺ or ↻ buttons to rotate the photo 90° left or right if it's tilted.
  3. Upload Back Side: Click the right slot and choose your back-side photo.
  4. Adjust Corners (if needed): Tap "Adjust Corners" and drag the four blue circles to the card corners.
  5. Choose Layout & Size: Side by Side or Stacked; Actual or Large.
  6. Apply Filters: Use Auto, B&W Doc, or Whiteboard to sharpen the output.
  7. Download PDF: Save the print-ready A4 PDF at 300 DPI. Print at 100% scale.

Rotate Feature

Photos taken on phones are often landscape when they should be portrait, or vice versa. The rotate buttons (↺ = 90° left, ↻ = 90° right) let you fix orientation in a single click. Each click rotates the photo 90°. So two clicks in the same direction give you a 180° rotation.

Best Results — Tips

  • Photograph the card on a dark matte surface in daylight. Avoid flash and glare.
  • Keep the card flat and hold the camera parallel to the card.
  • Use the rotate buttons before adjusting corners — it's faster.
  • Use B&W Doc filter for text-heavy scans to make text crisp and background white.
  • Print at "Actual Size" or "100% Scale" — never "Fit to Page".

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this 2 in 1 ID Maker free?

Yes, 100% free. No signup, no email, no daily limit.

Are my photos uploaded to any server?

Never. All processing runs in your browser using Canvas and pdf-lib.

How do I rotate a photo?

Use the ↺ (rotate left) or ↻ (rotate right) buttons below each upload slot. Each click rotates 90°. Two clicks give 180°.

How do I crop the card from a full photo?

Tap "Adjust Corners". Drag the four blue circles to the corners of the card. Then tap "Crop".

What size PDF does it produce?

Standard A4 size — 210 × 297 mm at 300 DPI.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. After the page loads once, disconnect from the internet and everything still works.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. Fully responsive with touch-friendly editor and rotate buttons.
