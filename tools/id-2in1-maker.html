2 in 1 ID Maker with Auto-Crop — Aadhaar, PAN Front aur Back ek hi Page par (2026)

Har Indian institution — banks, government portals, exam authorities, landlords, employers — aapke ID card ki copy maangti hai. Aur lagbhag sab ek hi page par chahte hain. DocEasy ka 2 in 1 ID Maker with Auto-Crop aapke front aur back photos ko automatically crop karke, ek clean, print-ready A4 page par merge kar deta hai — seconds mein, puri tarah browser ke andar.

Auto-Crop Kya Hai — Aur Kyun Zaroori Hai

Jab aap phone se Aadhaar card ki photo lete hain, to usually card table par rakha hota hai, aur photo mein bahut background bhi aa jaata hai. Agar aap isi photo ko merge kar dein, to output mein card chhota aur background zyada dikhega. Auto-Crop is problem ko solve karta hai — tool khud card ke 4 corners detect karta hai, background hataata hai, aur perspective correct karke exact CR80 size mein crop karta hai.

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai — Technical Detail

Ye tool OpenCV.js use karta hai — Google ki computer vision library, jo JavaScript mein compiled hai. Photo upload karte hi ye steps chalte hain:

  1. Grayscale conversion — image ko black-and-white mein convert
  2. Gaussian blur — noise hataata hai
  3. Canny edge detection — card ki boundaries find karta hai
  4. Contour detection — sabse bada rectangle dhundta hai (yahi card hai)
  5. Perspective transform — tirchhe corners ko straight karta hai
  6. Warp + Crop — final card image banata hai

Ye sab 2-3 seconds mein hota hai, aapke device par, bina kisi server ke.
