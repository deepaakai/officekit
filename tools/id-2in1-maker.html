2 in 1 ID Maker — Aadhaar, PAN Front aur Back ek hi Page par (2026 Guide)

Har Indian institution — banks, government portals, exam authorities, landlords, employers — aapke ID card ki copy maangti hai. Aur lagbhag sab ek hi page par chahte hain. Lekin jab aap Aadhaar ya PAN card ka front aur back photograph karte hain, wo do alag images ban jaati hain, aur printing mein do pages lag jaate hain. DocEasy ka 2 in 1 ID Maker isi problem ko solve karta hai — aapke front aur back photos ko ek clean, print-ready A4 page par merge kar deta hai, seconds mein, puri tarah browser ke andar.

Ye Tool Kyun Zaroori Hai

Socho ki jab aap bank ya government office mein do-page KYC documents jama karte hain, to kya hota hai. Doosra page transit mein kho jaata hai. Do pages galat order mein staple ho jaate hain. Clerk ko samajh nahi aata kaunsa back-side kis front-side ka hai. Ye sab friction, delays, aur rejection paida karta hai.

Dono sides ko ek page par combine karke aap:

  • Pages alag hone ya mis-match hone ka risk khatam kar dete hain
  • Paper usage 50% kam kar dete hain
  • Receiving clerk ke liye document process karna aasan bana dete hain
  • 90% Indian portals aur offices ke exact format se match karte hain
  • Time bachate hain — koi cutting, pasting, ya manual cropping nahi

Kis-Kis ID Card ko Merge Kar Sakte Hain?

Ye tool kisi bhi ID card ke saath kaam karta hai jo standard CR80 size (85.6 × 54 mm) mein hai — wahi size jo credit card ka hota hai. Isme shamil hain:

  • Aadhaar Card — UIDAI dwara jari
  • PAN Card — Income Tax Department dwara jari
  • Voter ID — Election Commission dwara jari
  • Driving Licence — state RTOs dwara jari
  • Shram Card / Labour Card
  • Ration Card (card format)
  • Ayushman Bharat Card
  • Health Insurance Cards
  • Bank-issued ID cards
  • Employee / Student ID cards

Agar aap apne card ka front aur back photograph kar sakte hain, to ye tool unhe beautifully merge kar dega.

Do Layout Options — Kab Kya Use Karein

Side by Side (A4 Landscape)

Do cards left aur right mein A4 landscape sheet par rakhe jaate hain. Ye sabse common layout hai KYC submissions, bank account openings, aur government forms ke liye kyunki isme dono sides clearly visible rehti hain aur compare karna aasan hota hai. Aadhaar, PAN, aur Voter ID submissions ke liye recommended.

Stacked Vertical (A4 Portrait)

Do cards ek doosre ke upar A4 portrait sheet par rakhe jaate hain. Ye layout tab preferred hai jab receiving office document ko portrait orientation mein chahti hai — jaise kuch insurance claims, passport applications, aur legal filings. Driving Licence submissions aur un documents ke liye recommended jahan portrait paper mandatory ho.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Step 1 — Front Side Upload karein: Left slot par click karein aur apni front-side photo choose karein (JPG, PNG, ya koi bhi standard image format). Image turant browser mein load ho jaayegi.
  2. Step 2 — Back Side Upload karein: Right slot par click karein aur back-side photo choose karein. Dono images ab preview mein dikhengi.
  3. Step 3 — Layout Choose karein: "Side by Side" chunein A4 landscape output ke liye, ya "Stacked Vertical" A4 portrait output ke liye.
  4. Step 4 — Card Size Choose karein: "Actual Size" standard CR80 cards (85.6 × 54 mm) ke liye, ya "Large" agar thoda bada card chahiye easy reading ke liye.
  5. Step 5 — Watermark (optional): Agar aap chaahte hain ki "DocEasy.org" ka chhota watermark bottom-right corner par aaye, to checkbox tick karein. Bank ya government submission ke liye OFF rakhein.
  6. Step 6 — Preview Check karein: Live preview exactly dikhata hai merged page kaisa lagega. Dono cards visible, straight, aur legible hone chahiye.
  7. Step 7 — Download ya Print karein: "Download as PDF" par click karein highest quality ke liye (300 DPI), "Download as JPG" shareable image ke liye, ya "Print Now" direct print ke liye.

Merging Kaise Hoti Hai — Andar Ki Baat

Jab aap dono images upload karte hain, tool puri tarah aapke browser ke andar chalta hai — HTML5 Canvas aur pdf-lib use karke. Yahan step-by-step kya hota hai:

  1. Dono images browser memory mein load hoti hain.
  2. Ek high-resolution canvas banaya jaata hai exactly A4 dimensions par (3508 × 2480 px landscape ke liye, 2480 × 3508 px portrait ke liye — 300 DPI par).
  3. Dono images canvas par correct card size par draw ki jaati hain, beech mein comfortable gap ke saath.
  4. Agar watermark ON hai, to "DocEasy.org" chhota text bottom-right corner par draw hota hai.
  5. PDF export ke liye, canvas ko pdf-lib use karke correctly-sized A4 PDF page mein embed kiya jaata hai.
  6. JPG export ke liye, canvas high-quality JPEG ke roop mein export hota hai.

Kisi bhi point par koi image server par upload nahi hoti. Sab kuch local hai. Aap khud verify kar sakte hain — Developer Tools kholkar Network tab dekhein, zero outgoing requests carry your images.

300 DPI Output Kyun Zaroori Hai

Zyadatar KYC offices aur government portals 200–300 DPI par ID card copies maangte hain. Kam DPI (72 ya 96) par, card ka text blurry ho jaata hai, QR codes scan hona band ho jaate hain, aur chhoti details (jaise Aadhaar ke last few digits) smudge ho jaati hain. DocEasy ka 2 in 1 ID Maker true 300 DPI par output render karta hai, ensuring ki har detail razor-sharp rahe jab kisi bhi inkjet ya laser printer par print ho.

Watermark — Kyun aur Kab Use Karein

Watermark ek chhota optional feature hai jo aapki output file par "DocEasy.org" ka text bottom-right corner mein add karta hai. Iske fayde:

  • Brand recall: Jab aap file kisi aur ko bhejte hain, unhe pata chalta hai kaunse tool se banaya.
  • Free promotion: Har PDF ke saath aapka naam viral hota hai.
  • Credibility: Kuch users professional-looking output pasand karte hain.

Kab OFF rakhein: Bank KYC, government portals, exam applications, passport submissions, aur koi bhi official document jahan extra text allowed nahi hai. Default setting OFF hai — aap khud chunein.

Best Results Ke Liye Tips

  • Scanner use karein agar possible ho: Flatbed scanner 300 DPI par sabse clean images deta hai. Phone photos bhi achhi kaam karti hain agar bright, even lighting mein li gayi hon.
  • Shadows aur glare se bachein: Card ko matte dark surface par daylight mein photograph karein. Camera flash se bachein — isse hot spots bante hain jo printed text chhupa dete hain.
  • Card flat rakhein: Benta ya curved card text ko distort karta hai. Photograph se pehle kisi book ke neeche thodi der flatten karein.
  • Background crop kar dein: Pehle hamare ID Card Cropper se background hata dein taaki sirf card dikhe.
  • Merging se pehle dono sides check karein: 200% zoom karke verify karein card number, photo, aur QR code readable hain.
  • Sahi layout chunein: Side-by-side safer default hai. Sirf tab stacked use karein jab receiving office explicitly portrait orientation maange.
  • Plain A4 paper par print karein: Regular 75–90 GSM paper perfectly kaam karta hai. Photo paper ya special cardstock ki zaroorat nahi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Kya ye 2 in 1 ID Maker bilkul free hai?

Haan — 100% free forever. No signup, no email, no watermark, no daily limit. Jitni baar chahiye use karein.

Kya meri ID card photos kisi server par upload hoti hain?

Kabhi nahi. Sab merging locally aapke browser mein hoti hai using Canvas aur pdf-lib. Aapki images kabhi device se bahar nahi jaati. Aap khud verify kar sakte hain Developer Tools → Network tab se.

Kaunse file formats supported hain?

Ye tool koi bhi image format accept karta hai jo aapka browser khol sakta hai: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC (Safari), aur more. Best results ke liye JPG ya PNG use karein.

Kya main do se zyada images merge kar sakta/sakti hoon?

Current version exactly do images support karta hai (front + back). Do se zyada combine karne ke liye, hamare Merge PDF tool se images ko PDF mein convert karke merge karein.

Kaunsa size PDF produce hota hai?

Standard A4 size — 210 × 297 mm (portrait) ya 297 × 210 mm (landscape) at 300 DPI. Indian banks, government portals, aur exam authorities universally accept karte hain.

Kya main card size choose kar sakta/sakti hoon?

Haan — "Actual Size" standard CR80 (85.6 × 54 mm) deta hai, aur "Large" 100 × 63 mm deta hai for easier reading.

Watermark kya hai aur kab use karein?

Watermark ek chhota optional text hai jo "DocEasy.org" bottom-right corner mein add karta hai. Ye default OFF hai. Bank ya government submissions ke liye OFF rakhein. Casual sharing ke liye ON kar sakte hain.

Kya watermark hata sakte hain?

Bilkul. Watermark checkbox default OFF hai. Aap chahein to tick karein, warna bilkul clean output milega — koi branding nahi.

Kya output normal printer par kaam karega?

Haan. PDF kisi bhi inkjet ya laser printer par A4 paper ke saath perfectly print hota hai. Koi special settings nahi chahiye.

Kya bina internet ke merge kar sakte hain?

Haan. Page ek baar load hone ke baad, aap internet disconnect karke bhi merge kar sakte hain. Sab locally chalta hai.

Kya ye mobile phones par kaam karta hai?

Haan. Tool fully responsive hai aur Android phones, iPhones, aur tablets par kaam karta hai. Apne phone gallery se photos upload karein aur merged PDF instantly download karein.

Merge karne ke baad cards readable hain ye kaise ensure karein?

Pehle hamare ID Card Cropper se har side ko precisely crop karein 300 DPI par. Phir clean crops ko merge karein for sharpest output.

Kya cards rotate kar sakte hain agar sideways hain?

Current version mein rotation nahi hai. Source photos ko upload se pehle kisi photo editor se rotate karein, ya ID Card Cropper use karein jo rotation include karta hai.

Kya mera ID card different size ka hai to kya hoga?

Zyadatar Indian ID cards CR80 standard (85.6 × 54 mm) follow karte hain. Agar aapka different hai, to "Large" option use karein ya upload se pehle manually crop karein.

Kya file size limit hai?

Koi hard limit nahi. Sirf constraint aapke device ki RAM hai. Har image 20 MB tak comfortably handle ho jaati hai on any modern phone or computer.

Kya merged output download ke baad edit kar sakte hain?

Haan. Output standard PDF ya JPG file hai — aap ise kisi bhi editor mein khol kar further changes kar sakte hain.
