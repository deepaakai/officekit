2 in 1 ID Maker with Scan-Style Auto-Crop — Aadhaar, PAN Front aur Back ek hi Page par (2026)

Har Indian institution — banks, government portals, exam authorities, landlords, employers — aapke ID card ki copy maangti hai. Aur lagbhag sab ek hi page par chahte hain. DocEasy ka 2 in 1 ID Maker aapke front aur back photos ko automatically crop karke, ek clean, print-ready A4 page par merge kar deta hai — seconds mein, puri tarah browser ke andar.

Is Tool Mein Kya Kya Hai

  • Auto-crop — card ke 4 corners khud detect hote hain aur perspective seedha ho jaata hai.
  • 4-corner manual crop — auto-crop galat ho to chaaron corners drag karke theek karein. Zoom loupe se corner exact pakad sakte hain.
  • Hamesha horizontal card — photo vertical ho ya tirchhi, card output landscape mein hi banega. Ulta dikhe to ek tap mein 180° flip.
  • Scan filters — Auto, Magic Color, Grayscale, B&W Doc aur Whiteboard.
  • Brightness, Contrast, Sharpness — contrast kam ya zyada karein, front aur back ko alag ya ek saath.
  • Print-ready — A4 landscape ya portrait, 300 DPI, PDF / JPG / direct print.

Auto-Crop Kya Hai — Aur Kyun Zaroori Hai

Jab aap phone se Aadhaar card ki photo lete hain, to usually card table par rakha hota hai, aur photo mein bahut background bhi aa jaata hai. Agar aap isi photo ko merge kar dein, to output mein card chhota aur background zyada dikhega. Auto-Crop is problem ko solve karta hai — tool khud card ke 4 corners detect karta hai, background hataata hai, aur perspective correct karke exact CR80 size mein crop karta hai.

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai — Technical Detail

Ye tool OpenCV.js se card detect karta hai — computer vision library jo JavaScript mein compiled hai. Photo upload karte hi ye steps chalte hain:

  1. Grayscale + Gaussian blur — noise hataata hai
  2. Edge aur threshold detection — card ki boundaries find karta hai
  3. Contour detection — sabse sahi 4-kone wala shape (yahi card hai) chunta hai
  4. Perspective transform — tirchhe corners ko straight karta hai
  5. Landscape orientation — card ko horizontal karke 85.6 × 54 mm ratio mein crop karta hai
  6. Scan filter — levels, contrast aur background cleanup apply hota hai

Ye sab aapke device par hota hai, bina kisi server ke.

Frequently Asked Questions

Kya ye 2 in 1 ID Maker free hai?

Haan — 100% free. No signup, no email, no daily limit. "DocEasy.org" watermark optional hai aur default par band rehta hai.

Auto-crop kaise kaam karta hai?

OpenCV.js se card ke 4 corners detect hote hain, perspective correct hoti hai, aur card hamesha horizontal CR80 size mein crop hota hai.

Agar auto-crop galat ho ya fail ho jaye to?

Tool corner editor khol deta hai (ya aap "Corners adjust" dabayein). Chaaron blue circles ko card ke kono par drag karein aur "Crop karein" dabayein. Mobile par drag karte waqt zoom loupe dikhta hai.

Card ulta dikh raha hai, kya karein?

Us side ke neeche "Ulta? 180° flip" button dabayein.

Contrast kam ya zyada kaise karein?

"Scan Filters & Adjust" mein Contrast slider use karein. Dono sides ko ek saath ya sirf Front / Back ko alag adjust kar sakte hain.

Meri photos upload hoti hain?

Kabhi nahi. Sab processing aapke browser mein hoti hai. Photos device se bahar nahi jaati.

Kaunsa size PDF produce hota hai?

Standard A4 size — 210 × 297 mm (portrait) ya 297 × 210 mm (landscape) at 300 DPI.
