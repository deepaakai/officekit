How to Use the Randomizer Tools

Our tool includes three different randomizers. Here is how you can use each one:

1. Random Number Generator

Select the "Random Number" tab. Enter your minimum and maximum values (e.g., 1 and 100). You can also choose how many random numbers you want to generate at once. Click "Generate Number" to get your result instantly. This is great for picking a random roll number, a lottery ticket, or just for math games.

2. Virtual Dice Roller

Select the "Dice Roller" tab. Choose how many dice you want to roll (up to 6) and the type of dice. If you are playing Ludo or Snakes & Ladders, keep the standard D6 (1-6). If you are playing Dungeons & Dragons (D&D), you can choose D4, D8, D10, D12, or D20. Click "Roll Dice" to see the animation and the total score.

3. Lottery / Name Picker

Select the "Lottery Picker" tab. Type or paste your list of names or items, one per line. Enter how many winners you want to pick. Click "Pick Winner", and the tool will randomly select a name from your list. This is perfect for YouTube giveaways or classroom activities.

Why Use Our Random Generator?

Many websites use basic JavaScript `Math.random()` which is not truly random and can be predictable. Our tool uses the Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG) built into your browser. This means the results are highly secure and perfectly fair.

  • Fairness: Every number or name has an exactly equal chance of being picked.
  • Privacy: The generation happens inside your browser. No data is sent to our servers, so your names and numbers stay private.
  • No Bias: Computers are better than humans at being random. When you pick a number "in your head," you often subconsciously pick your favorite numbers. Our tool has zero bias.

Types of Dice (For Gamers)

If you are a tabletop gamer, you know that different games require different dice. Here is what we offer:

  • D6 (Six-sided): The most common dice used in board games like Ludo, Monopoly, and Yahtzee.
  • D20 (Twenty-sided): The iconic dice used in Dungeons & Dragons for attack rolls and skill checks.
  • D10 and D12: Used for specific damage rolls and character stats in role-playing games.
