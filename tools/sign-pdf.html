Is a Digital Signature Created Here Legally Valid?

This is a crucial question. The tool creates an electronic signature (e-signature), not a certified Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).

  • Electronic Signature (e-Signature): This is an image of your handwritten signature placed on a document. It is legally valid for the vast majority of everyday use cases: freelance contracts, NDAs, invoices, internal approvals, and rental agreements. Under the IT Act of 2000 in India and the ESIGN Act in the US, e-signatures are legally binding as long as both parties agree to the electronic format.
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC): This is a cryptographically secure signature issued by a government authority (like Capricorn, eMudhra). It includes a token and is required for filing official government documents (like Income Tax returns, MCA filings, or tender submissions).

If you are signing a standard contract, our tool is perfect. If you are filing official government paperwork, you will need a DSC token.

Tips for the Best Signature Quality

  • Use a Transparent PNG: If you are uploading a signature, make sure it has a transparent background. A signature with a white background will cover the text underneath it.
  • Draw with a Stylus: If you are using a touch device, a stylus provides much better control than a finger.
  • Don't Make it Too Large: Scale the signature so it looks natural on the page. It should be roughly the same size as a signature you would write with a pen.

Yes. Use the page controls at the top of the PDF preview to navigate to the page you want to sign. The signature will be placed on the currently visible page.

We recommend files under 25MB. Larger files will still work, but might take a few extra seconds to process depending on your device's speed.
