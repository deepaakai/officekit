Frequently Asked Questions

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every step — reading, detecting, processing, and muxing — happens inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

How does automatic speaker detection work?

The audio track is decoded and split into short windows. DocEasy measures loudness to find where someone is speaking, then estimates each segment's fundamental pitch. Segments with similar average pitch are grouped together into "Speaker 1," "Speaker 2," etc.

What is muxing?

Muxing is the process of combining the newly generated audio track with the original video track into a single video file. We use FFmpeg.wasm (a browser version of FFmpeg) to do this without uploading your files.

Why do big pitch shifts sound a little "phasey"?

DocEasy uses a phase vocoder implemented in plain JavaScript. This works well for shifts within ±1 octave. Push it further and you'll start to hear phase-vocoder artifacts. This is an inherent trade-off of doing real pitch shifting in the browser.
