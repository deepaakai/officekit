How to Use the BMI Calculator

This tool is designed for people all over the world, allowing you to mix different units of measurement. Here is how to use it:

Step 1: Select Your Standard

Click the "Standard: Asian/Indian" button at the top. If you are of Asian descent, keep the Asian Standard. If you are from Europe, Africa, or the Americas, select the WHO International Standard.

Step 2: Enter Your Weight

Type your weight into the box and use the dropdown next to it to choose either kg (kilograms) or lbs (pounds).

Step 3: Enter Your Height

Type your height. If you use centimeters, keep the dropdown as cm. If you know your height in feet and inches, change the dropdown to ft/in, and two boxes will appear for you to enter both.

Step 4: Click Calculate

Press the "Calculate BMI" button. The tool will instantly show your BMI, your health category, your healthy weight range, and how much weight you should lose or gain.

Why Are There Two Different Standards?

Body composition is different all over the world. Research has shown that people of Asian descent tend to have a higher percentage of body fat at the same weight and height compared to people of European descent. This puts them at a higher risk of health issues like diabetes at a lower weight. Therefore, the Asian standard flags "overweight" at a BMI of 23, while the International standard flags it at 25.

Limitations of BMI

While BMI is very useful for a quick check, it is not perfect. It does not distinguish between muscle and fat. Bodybuilders may have a high BMI while being very fit. It also doesn't show where the fat is located (belly fat is more dangerous). You should use BMI as a general guide, not a medical diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use kg and feet together?

Yes, absolutely. We designed this tool so you can select the unit for weight and height independently. This is especially useful in India where weight is often measured in kg and height in feet/inches.

Is this BMI calculator free to use?

Yes, it is 100% free. You can check your BMI as many times as you want. There are no hidden charges and no sign-up required.

What is a good BMI for a woman?

The healthy BMI range is the same for both men and women based on the standard you select. For the Asian standard, 18.5 to 22.9 is considered healthy.
