1. United States: Masking Social Security Numbers (SSN)

In the United States, your 9-digit Social Security Number (SSN) is the primary key to your financial profile, credit score, tax filings, and medical records. Exposing your complete SSN allows identity criminals to open fraudulent lines of credit, claim synthetic tax refunds, or compromise employment accounts.

Under US privacy standards (such as HIPAA in healthcare and federal court redaction rules):

  • The Standard Format: Always conceal the first 5 digits, revealing only the final 4 digits (e.g., XXX-XX-1234).
  • State Driver’s Licenses: Redact the unique driver license number and barcode when verifying age or residential address for non-governmental verifiers.
  • US Passports: Redact the 9-digit passport book number and the bottom Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) when providing travel confirmation to unauthorized third parties.

2. United Kingdom: National Insurance (NINO) & Driving Licenses

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, personal data shared with landlords (Right to Rent checks) or employers must follow the principle of data minimization.

  • National Insurance Number (NINO): Comprising 2 prefix letters, 6 numbers, and a suffix letter (e.g., QQ 12 34 56 A). Redact the central 6 numeric digits before transmitting payslips or utility bills.
  • UK Driver’s License: A 16-character alphanumeric string containing your surname and birth details. Conceal the specific license serial digits to prevent loan fraud.

3. Canada: Social Insurance Number (SIN) Redaction

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) explicitly warns that private-sector organizations cannot legally demand your 9-digit Social Insurance Number (SIN) except for specific income reporting scenarios (such as T4 tax slips). When presenting Canadian proof of identity for housing, telecom subscriptions, or car financing, always redact the entire 9-digit SIN string.

4. Australia: Tax File Number (TFN) & Medicare Cards

The Australian Privacy Act strictly regulates how your 9-digit Tax File Number (TFN) can be collected. Unlawfully requesting or retaining an unmasked TFN carries severe federal penalties. When sharing pay summaries or superannuation statements, black out the TFN digits completely. Similarly, when sharing Australian Medicare cards, conceal the individual's reference line unless verifying medical treatment directly.

5. India: UIDAI Compliant Masked Aadhaar, PAN & RC

In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and UIDAI directives mandate that individuals should never distribute unmasked photocopies of their 12-digit Aadhaar card:

  • Masked Aadhaar: The first 8 digits are obscured, presenting only the last 4 digits (e.g., XXXX-XXXX-1234). Our visual tool allows you to place a solid black rectangle over both the 8 printed digits and the embedded QR code.
  • PAN (Permanent Account Number): Hide the primary alphanumeric sequence when using PAN as photo identification for non-tax transactions.
  • Driving License & RC (Vehicle Registration): Redact vehicle chassis numbers, engine numbers, and DL license codes before listing vehicles on secondary marketplaces or sharing with mechanics.
