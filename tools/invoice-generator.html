Understanding Indian GST for 2025-26

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a destination-based tax levied on the supply of goods and services. For a valid GST invoice in India, the tax breakdown is mandatory based on whether the transaction is Intra-State (within the same state) or Inter-State (between two different states).

1. Intra-State Supply (CGST + SGST)

If you are in Maharashtra and your client is also in Maharashtra, you must charge:

  • CGST (Central GST): Goes to the Central Government.
  • SGST (State GST): Goes to the State Government.

For example, if the GST rate for your service is 18%, you will charge 9% CGST and 9% SGST. Our tool automatically splits the tax for you.

2. Inter-State Supply (IGST)

If you are in Delhi and your client is in Karnataka, you must charge IGST (Integrated GST). The full 18% tax goes to the Central Government, which later distributes it to the destination state. You do not split the tax here.

3. Mandatory GST Invoice Fields

To ensure your invoice is valid for Input Tax Credit (ITC), the following details must be present:

  • Your GSTIN (GST Identification Number).
  • Client's GSTIN (if they are a registered business).
  • A unique, sequential invoice number (e.g., INV-2025-001).
  • Date of issue.
  • HSN Code or SAC Code (Services Accounting Code) for the items/services provided.
  • Taxable value, CGST, SGST, or IGST amounts clearly separated.
  • Total invoice value (in words is recommended).

Invoicing International Clients (Export of Services)

If you are an Indian freelancer billing a client in the US, UK, or Europe, this falls under "Export of Services". In India, export of services is generally a Zero-Rated Supply.

  • You usually do not charge GST on the invoice.
  • However, you must mention the "Place of Supply" (e.g., United States).
  • You must use the LUT (Letter of Undertaking) reference number on the invoice to avoid paying IGST upfront.
  • You can accept payment in USD, EUR, GBP, etc. Our tool allows you to switch the currency symbol instantly.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST and SGST are applied for sales within the same state (split 50/50). IGST is applied for sales to a different state or Union Territory.

Do I need to add HSN/SAC codes in the invoice?

Yes, for GST compliance, it is mandatory to mention HSN (for goods) or SAC (for services) codes. Our tool allows you to enter these in the description field.
