1. Statutory Tax Anatomy: Decoding CGST, SGST, IGST, and UTGST

Under the Indian Goods and Services Tax framework, tax jurisdiction is determined strictly by the physical relationship between the Location of the Supplier and the Place of Supply:

Transaction ClassificationGeographic RelationshipApplicable Tax LevyRevenue Allocation
Intra-State SupplySupplier State == Place of SupplyCGST + SGSTDivided equally: 50% to Central Government, 50% to State Government.
Inter-State SupplySupplier State != Place of SupplyIGST100% collected by Central Government and apportioned to destination State.
Union Territory SupplyInside a UT without a LegislatureCGST + UTGST50% to Central Government, 50% to Union Territory administration.

The DocEasy engine evaluates your supplier state against the recipient's billing coordinates and designated Place of Supply in real time. If both parties reside in Delhi, an 18% levy is automatically apportioned into 9% CGST and 9% SGST. If the invoice crosses state borders to Maharashtra, the entire 18% accumulates under IGST.

2. Mandatory Statutory Fields for B2B Invoices Under Section 31

According to Section 31 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act read alongside Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, every valid tax invoice issued to a registered business must contain the following structural fields:

  • Consecutive Sequential Numbering: Must contain only alphabets, numerals, or special characters (hyphens and slashes), unique for each financial year.
  • Date of Issuance: Documenting the exact date the invoice is raised.
  • Supplier Identification: Name, registered operational address, and valid 15-digit GSTIN.
  • Recipient Identification: Name, address, and GSTIN (or UIN) of the recipient.
  • HSN / SAC Code: Harmonized System of Nomenclature code for physical goods, or Services Accounting Code for professional services.
  • Place of Supply: Accompanied by the two-digit State Code (e.g., Delhi: 07, Maharashtra: 27, Karnataka: 29).
  • Taxable Value & Surcharges: Clear itemization showing unit rates, applicable discounts, and distinct tax rate tiers.
  • Signature or Digital Seal: Authorized signatory signature or clear electronic attribution.

3. HSN and SAC Code Regulations (2026 Thresholds)

To avoid invoice rejection during statutory reconciliation, businesses must adhere to national HSN/SAC reporting standards based on Aggregate Annual Turnover (AATO):

  • Turnover up to ₹5 Crore (B2B): Minimum 4-digit HSN code mandatory.
  • Turnover exceeding ₹5 Crore: Mandatory 6-digit HSN code across all B2B and B2C commercial invoices.
  • Import & Export Transactions: Full 8-digit HSN classification mandatory.
  • Service Sector Providers: Standard 6-digit SAC codes (e.g., 998314 for IT and software design services).

Q1: How do I export my invoice as an official PDF file?

Click the PDF button on the top toolbar or press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac). The browser's native print interface will open. Select "Save as PDF" as your destination, verify that Paper Size is set to A4, ensure "Background Graphics" is checked, and click Save.

Q2: Does DocEasy store a backup of my invoices if I clear my browser history?

Because DocEasy operates with 100% client-side privacy, your saved invoice records are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage). If you manually wipe your browser cookies and site storage, that history will be removed. We recommend saving finished invoices as PDF files to your local drive for permanent accounting archives.

Q3: How does the automatic Number-to-Words currency conversion work?

Our engine incorporates a standardized currency translation algorithm that computes integer and fractional figures into Indian numbering terms (Lakhs and Crores for INR) or international terms (Millions and Billions for USD/EUR), automatically outputting compliant textual totals (e.g., "Twenty-Five Thousand Four Hundred Rupees Only").

Q4: Can I issue invoices in foreign currencies like USD, EUR, or AED?

Yes. Select your preferred currency from the Edit form dropdown. The tool automatically adapts currency symbols and textual conversions across the document layout.

Q5: What is the purpose of the 'Computer Generated Invoice' disclaimer?

Under modern commercial law and IT Act provisions, digitally transmitted invoices that carry an explicit note stating "This is a computer-generated invoice and does not require a physical signature" are legally valid for commercial trade and audit reconciliation.

Q6: What image formats should I use for company logos and signatures?

We recommend using transparent PNG files. Transparent backgrounds blend seamlessly into all 8 template color schemes without leaving awkward white border boxes around your logo or signature.

Q7: Can I apply custom GST rates to different line items on the same invoice?

Yes. Each row in the item table features an independent GST percentage field. You can combine 0% exempted items, 5% essential goods, and 18% professional services within a single invoice.

Q8: How does the Round-Off toggle function?

When set to "Auto", the calculator automatically rounds fractional decimal totals to the nearest whole rupee, adjusting the difference under a designated "Round Off" line item.

Q9: Can I use this generator for non-GST or composite dealer billing?

Yes. Simply set the Tax Mode dropdown to "No Tax" or configure item GST fields to 0%. This formats the document as a clean Bill of Supply or commercial debit note.

Q10: Is there any limit on how many line items I can add to an invoice?

There are no arbitrary software limits. You can add as many rows as needed. For multi-page documents, the browser print engine will automatically distribute items across consecutive A4 pages.
