Complete Guide to Government Photo & Signature Sizes in India (2026)

If you are applying for a government job, competitive exam, or an official document like a PAN card or Driving Licence, you have definitely faced the frustrating "Invalid File Size" or "Dimensions Exceeded" error. Portals like SSC, UPSC, NTA, IBPS, and Sarathi use automated validation scripts that instantly reject any file that does not strictly match their exact requirements.

Whether you are uploading a photo for NEET UG, a signature for IBPS PO, or an ID photo for a PAN card, the dimensions and file size must be exact. 1. Why Do Government Portals Have Strict Size Limits?

Every year, millions of candidates apply for various central and state government exams. If the servers allowed high-resolution images (e.g., 2 MB), the servers would crash within hours. To handle millions of applications simultaneously, the National Informatics Centre (NIC) and other exam authorities set strict limits on file sizes (usually between 10 KB to 300 KB) and dimensions.

Furthermore, these photos are printed on admit cards and official verification rolls. Therefore, the portals also require a minimum resolution (usually 200 to 300 DPI) to ensure your face is clearly visible when printed.

3. Understanding Units: Pixels vs. Centimeters vs. Millimeters

Many candidates get confused between cm, mm, and pixels. Here is a quick guide to standard conversions used by government portals (at 300 DPI print quality):

  • 3.5 cm is roughly equal to 413 pixels.
  • 4.5 cm is roughly equal to 531 pixels.
  • 1 inch equals exactly 300 pixels (at 300 DPI).

Our tool automatically handles these conversions. If a portal asks for 3.5 x 4.5 cm, you can simply select 'cm' from the unit dropdown, and the tool will generate the exact pixel dimensions for you.

4. How to Create a Valid Signature for Government Exams

Your signature is just as important as your photo. Here are some tips to ensure it gets accepted:

  • Always sign on a white piece of paper with a black or blue pen.
  • Make sure the signature is neat and fits inside the specified box (usually 4cm x 2cm).
  • Do not use a marker or thick pen, as it may bleed and make the file size larger.
  • Upload the photo of the signature to our tool and set the target KB to 20KB.
