Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF editor completely free?

Yes — 100% free with no signup, no email, no subscription, no daily limit, and no watermark. Every tool and every feature is available to every visitor. Use it as many times as you like, forever.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

Never. Every operation runs inside your browser's memory using PDF.js and PDF-Lib. Your file is never transmitted, stored, or logged. You can verify this yourself by opening your browser's Developer Tools → Network tab, loading a PDF, and editing it — you will see zero outgoing requests carrying your document data. You can even turn off Wi-Fi and keep editing.

Can I edit a password-protected PDF?

Yes. When you open an encrypted PDF, the editor prompts you for the password. For e-Aadhaar PDFs, the password is usually the first 4 letters of your name in CAPITALS followed by your birth year — e.g., name Deepak, birth year 1998 → DEEP1998. For PAN card PDFs, it is your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format — e.g., DOB 15 January 1998 → 15011998. If the PDF is heavily encrypted, the editor will open it as a flattened version — you can still annotate on top, but the original text cannot be edited inline.

Can I sign a PDF without printing it?

Yes. Upload a transparent PNG of your signature (scan it on white paper and remove the background, or write it with a stylus on a blank canvas), click the Image button, position it on the signature line, resize as needed, and save. This produces a legally-recognised electronic signature for most Indian and international documents.

Does the Undo/Redo work properly?

Yes — full history tracking. Every add, delete, move, resize, rotate, and style change is recorded. Use the toolbar buttons or press Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) to redo. The history stack holds up to 50 actions.

Why does my Hindi or emoji text turn into "?" after saving?

PDF's built-in fonts (Helvetica, Times, Courier) only support Latin characters and a few extended symbols. Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Arabic, and emoji characters require embedding a Unicode font file. The current version uses only standard PDF fonts to keep the file size small and compatible with every PDF reader. Multi-line Hindi input is coming in a future update.

What file types can I insert as images?

PNG (with transparency support — perfect for signatures and logos) and JPG/JPEG. Other formats (WEBP, GIF, HEIC) should be converted to PNG or JPG first — you can use our Image Converter tool for that.

Does this work on mobile phones?

Yes. The editor is fully responsive with enlarged touch handles (24 pixels) and touch-friendly controls. All drag, resize, rotate, and delete gestures work with a finger. For the smoothest experience, use Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS.

Can I add a watermark to every page at once?

Yes. Click the Watermark button, type your text, set the opacity, and confirm when asked if you want to apply it to all pages. Every page will receive an identical watermark with a subtle diagonal rotation.

How many pages can I edit at once?

There is no hard limit — the editor handles PDFs with hundreds of pages. Performance depends on your device's memory. Thumbnail rendering happens progressively in the background, so you can start editing page 1 immediately while later pages load.

Can I delete pages from the PDF?

Not directly in this version — but you can use our Split PDF tool to extract specific page ranges, or our Merge PDF tool to combine files. Direct page deletion is planned for a future update.

Will my annotations look the same when printed?

Yes. All annotations are compiled into the output PDF using standard PDF primitives — text, vector rectangles, embedded images — so they look identical whether viewed on screen, printed on paper, or opened in Adobe Acrobat. Output resolution is 300 DPI (print-ready).
