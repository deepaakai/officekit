Free PDF Editor — Add Text, Signature & Annotations Online (2026)

Most PDFs were never meant to be edited — they were meant to be shared. But the moment you need to fill in a form, sign a contract, add a note, or place a stamp on a receipt, the PDF suddenly becomes a locked puzzle. Adobe Acrobat costs ₹1,500+ per month. Other online "PDF editors" upload your file to a server, watermark the output, or limit you to three documents per day. DocEasy's PDF Editor changes that — you get a clean, fast, private editing experience directly in your browser, at no cost.

This editor is built around a simple idea: most "PDF editing" tasks are actually annotation tasks — adding something on top of the existing page rather than rewriting the original text. That's exactly what this tool does. It lets you place text, signatures, images, stamps, watermarks, whiteout patches, rectangles, and circles anywhere on any page. Everything is saved as real, permanent content inside the output PDF.

What You Can Do With This Tool

  • Fill forms: Add text into blank PDF forms — government applications, job forms, invoices, tax forms, and more.
  • Sign documents: Insert your signature image (with transparent background) on any line or corner.
  • Add dates: Type today's date in any font, size, or color, exactly where it belongs.
  • Whiteout mistakes: Cover wrong text with a white box, then type the correct text on top.
  • Stamp documents: Apply APPROVED, REJECTED, PAID, VERIFIED, or a custom watermark in a single click.
  • Annotate: Draw red rectangles or circles around important sections.
  • Add images: Insert logos, photos, QR codes, or any PNG/JPG into any page.
  • Multi-page support: Add annotations to page 1, page 2, page 10 — everything is preserved in the final PDF.

How to Use — Step-by-Step

  1. Upload your PDF: Drag it into the upload zone, or click to browse. Password-protected PDFs (like e-Aadhaar, PAN, or bank statements) are supported — just enter the password when prompted.
  2. Add text: Click the Text button in the toolbar, then click anywhere on the PDF. A text box appears — start typing. Change font, size, bold, italic, or color from the toolbar.
  3. Add a signature: Click Image, upload your signature scan (a PNG with transparent background looks best), and drag it into position.
  4. Whiteout and correct: Click Whiteout, then click over the wrong text. Move the white box to cover it, then add new text on top.
  5. Add a stamp or watermark: Choose a stamp from the dropdown, or click Watermark to enter your own text and apply it to one page or every page.
  6. Move or delete: Drag any element to reposition it. Click to select, then press Delete or use the Delete button in the toolbar.
  7. Save: Click Save & Download PDF. The output PDF contains all your annotations permanently — no watermark, no extra file, no signup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF Editor completely free?

Yes — 100% free, no signup, no watermark, no daily limits. Use it as many times as you like.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

Never. Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device — you can even work offline after the page loads.

Can I edit the original text inside a PDF?

PDFs don't store text in an editable format like Word does — the original text is a drawing instruction. This tool is designed to add annotations on top: text, signatures, stamps, images, watermarks, and whiteout patches. For 90% of real-world "editing" tasks, this is exactly what you need.

Can I sign a PDF without printing it?

Yes. Upload your signature as a PNG (transparent background works best), use the Image button to place it on the signature line, resize and drag it into position, then save the PDF. This is a legally-recognised way to sign in most countries.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scanned PDFs are essentially images, so you can't edit the existing text, but you can add text, whiteout, images, stamps and watermarks on top. This is perfect for signing or annotating scanned documents.

What is the maximum file size?

There's no hard limit, but PDFs over 50 MB may slow down your browser depending on your device's memory. For most documents, PDFs up to 200 MB work smoothly.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The toolbar collapses neatly on phones, and all touch gestures (drag, select, delete) work perfectly on Android and iOS browsers.

Can I add text to a specific page only?

Yes. Use the page navigation buttons (Prev/Next) to go to any page, add your annotations, and they'll stay on that page in the final PDF.

Do I need to install anything?

No. This is a pure browser tool — no extension, no app, no plugin. Just open the page and start editing.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave, Opera — any modern browser from the last 3 years.
