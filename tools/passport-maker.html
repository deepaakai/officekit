Complete Step-by-Step Guide: How to Cut, Customize, and Print Multiple Passport Photos on One A4 Sheet

Whether you are a student applying for SSC, UPSC, or NEET, a job seeker preparing documents, or a cyber cafe operator printing sheets for multiple walk-in customers, following these steps lets you cut and arrange passport photos cleanly in under two minutes.

Step 1: Uploading Multiple Photos at Once

Start by clicking the upload area or dragging one or more portraits from your phone gallery, desktop folder, or camera. If you are preparing photos for a family or several cyber cafe customers, select multiple images together—or click Add photos inside the batch strip at any time to append additional people up to 12 portraits per batch. Every uploaded portrait appears in the interactive thumbnail strip at the top of the workspace. Beneath each thumbnail is an individual Copies field (ranging from 1 to 20, defaulting to 2) so you can assign exact quantities per person right away.

Step 2: AI Background Removal & Color Customization (Blue, White, Transparent)

Once your portraits load, the built-in AI segmentation model runs locally in your browser to separate hair, shoulders, and facial contours from cluttered room walls. Inside the Background tab, pick official studio shades such as Royal Blue, Sky Blue, Clean White, Passport Red, Neutral Gray, or any exact hex tone via the custom color picker. If you need a master cutout for ID card software or government portals that apply backgrounds automatically, select the checkerboard Transparent swatch to export a crisp PNG with an alpha channel. Use the Edge Feather slider to soften hair transitions naturally, or switch to Original BG whenever a photo was already captured against a clean studio backdrop.

Step 3: How to Cut and Resize to Exact Millimeter Dimensions

Different authorities reject photographs over even minor aspect ratio errors. Open the Size & Presets tab to switch instantly between standard international and Indian dimensions, including 35 × 45 mm (Indian Passport, SSC, Railway RRB, UPSC, NEET, JEE, Schengen Visa), 25 × 35 mm (PAN Card and Driving Licence), 20 × 25 mm (Stamp Size), 45 × 35 mm (IBPS landscape format), and 51 × 51 mm (2 × 2 inch US Visa). Need a specialized school or state-board size? Enter any custom width and height in millimeters and click Apply Custom Size. Every single export and print sheet maintains exact physical proportions at 300 DPI without stretching or distortion.

Step 4: Adding Name and Date in English, Hindi & Regional Languages

Competitive exams such as SSC, Railway, Army, and state recruitment boards frequently require the applicant's full name and the date of photograph printed cleanly at the bottom of the portrait. Open the Name & Date tab to configure a compact white caption strip drawn cleanly inside the bottom 8% to 12% of the photo height—eliminating oversized white banners that obscure the shoulders. Crucially, every person in your batch maintains completely independent caption settings. You can enable the caption for Person A ("Rahul Kumar"), enable a different name and date for Person B, and leave the toggle disabled for Person C so no strip appears on Person C's prints.

To add name in Hindi/regional languages to photo prints, select the matching script from the Font dropdown—such as Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi/Marathi/Sanskrit, Noto Sans Arabic or Noto Nastaliq Urdu for Urdu, Noto Sans Tamil, Noto Sans Telugu, Noto Sans Malayalam, Noto Sans Kannada, or Noto Sans for English and Cyrillic. Note that choosing a font controls script shaping and typography only (it does not auto-translate words), so type or paste your text in the target language using your device keyboard. When Urdu or Arabic script is selected, the tool automatically activates right-to-left (RTL) text direction so characters and word order render accurately. Adjust text size, text color, and left, center, or right alignment inside the vertically centered strip; the photo border is drawn last so it frames both the portrait and caption strip cleanly.

Step 5: Natural Skin Brightening, Zoom (Mouse Wheel & Pinch), and Position Lock

Before finalizing your crop, open the Beauty tab to apply natural face brightening without making portraits look artificial or blurred. Instead of applying a simple Gaussian blur across the whole image, the skin-aware YCbCr enhancement pipeline detects facial skin tones, lifts skin brightness naturally, reduces dark spots and blemishes, and smooths uneven texture while preserving sharp eyes, eyebrows, lips, hair strands, and keeping the background untouched.

Next, open the Position tab and toggle on the optional Head Guide overlay from the Background tab to ensure the face occupies 70% to 80% of frame height. On desktop computers, hover over the preview canvas and scroll your mouse wheel to zoom smoothly without scrolling the page. On mobile phones and tablets, use two-finger pinch-to-zoom directly on the canvas and single-finger drag to pan. Once the head and shoulders are centered accurately, turn on Lock Position to freeze zoom and drag gestures so accidental touches never shift your alignment while you edit other tabs.

Step 6: Batch Series Printing — Multiple People on One A4 Sheet

To print multiple passport photos on one A4 sheet without wasting expensive glossy paper, open the Print Sheet tab. Unlike basic tools that interleave portraits in an awkward alternating pattern, DocEasy arranges batches in strict consecutive Series order. For example, if Person A has 6 copies, Person B has 4 copies, and Person C (with captions disabled) has 8 copies, the sheet renders all 6 of Person A first, followed immediately by 4 of Person B, and then 8 of Person C—preserving every person's own background, framing, border, and caption.

The sheet engine starts from the top edge of the A4 page using small printer-safe margins (3 mm by default) and arranges photographs from left to right. In Auto (≤6) mode, the layout calculates exactly how many photos fit per row based on your chosen millimeter dimensions and A4 printable width, targeting up to 6 photos across the first row when dimensions allow (for instance, 6 PAN-size or stamp-size photos fit at 100% scale, whereas wider 51 × 51 mm US Visa photos automatically arrange at 3 per row instead of shrinking illegally). Leaving the unused lower portion of the A4 sheet blank makes it easy for photo studios to trim the top rows and reuse the remainder of the glossy paper later.

Step 7: Downloading and Printing at 300 DPI

Click Download Photo (300 DPI HD) to save an individual portrait with embedded 300 DPI print metadata, or click Generate Print Sheet followed by Download Print-Ready JPG (300 DPI) for the full A4 or 4×6 sheet. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a name and date to a passport photo online for free?

Upload your photo, open the Name & Date tab, turn on the toggle switch, and enter your full name and photograph date. The tool draws a compact 8% to 12% white strip cleanly at the bottom of the photograph with adjustable text size, color, font script, and alignment.

Can I print multiple different people on one A4 sheet with different copy counts?

Yes. Upload up to 12 different photos at once or click Add photos in the batch strip. Under each thumbnail, set the exact Copies number (1 to 20). The Print Sheet tab prints everyone in consecutive series order (for example, 6 copies of Person A, 4 of Person B, and 8 of Person C) on a single A4 page.

Can I enable captions for one person and disable them for another on the same sheet?

Yes. Every uploaded photo keeps completely independent Name & Date settings. Click any thumbnail in the batch strip and toggle Name & Date on or off for that specific individual without affecting any other photo in your batch.

Does this passport photo maker support Hindi, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, and other regional languages?

Yes. Built-in Google Noto Fonts support English, Hindi (Devanagari), Urdu (Arabic and Nastaliq with automatic right-to-left rendering), Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, and Cyrillic. Select the matching script from the font dropdown and type or paste your text in that language; note that the font selector styles characters and does not auto-translate English into another language.

Why does the A4 sheet place 5 or 6 photos in the first row starting from the top?

Starting rows from the top-left with small printer-safe margins saves paper so unused glossy paper below the printed rows can be trimmed cleanly and reused. In Auto mode, the layout targets up to 6 photos per row whenever physical dimensions fit inside the A4 printable width at 100% scale (such as 25×35 mm PAN or 20×25 mm stamp photos), and automatically reduces columns for wider sizes like 51×51 mm US Visa photos so images are never distorted or shrunk unfairly.

How does the Beauty / Natural Face Brightening feature work?

Unlike basic blur filters that smudge eyes and brighten walls, our Beautifier uses YCbCr color-space skin detection combined with edge-preserving smoothing and blemish reduction. It gently lifts facial skin brightness and evens skin tone while keeping eyes, eyebrows, lips, hair, and background completely sharp.

How do I zoom and lock my photo position on mobile and desktop?

On desktop, hover over the photo preview and roll your mouse wheel to zoom in or out; drag with the mouse to pan. On mobile phones, use two fingers to pinch-to-zoom and one finger to drag. Once your framing aligns with the Head Guide, enable Lock Position in the Position tab to prevent accidental movement.

What is the best way to cut passport photos after printing the A4 sheet?

Keep a thin 2–3 px black border enabled in the Border tab and set a 2 mm photo gap in the Print Sheet tab. Because rows align cleanly in straight horizontal and vertical grids from the top of the page, you can slice entire strips quickly using a steel ruler and craft knife or a desktop paper trimmer.

Are my photos uploaded to any cloud server?

No. All AI portrait segmentation, skin tone enhancement, text rendering, and 300 DPI JPEG/PNG generation run locally inside your browser. Your biometric photos never leave your device.

How do I download a transparent background PNG instead of a colored background?

Open the Background tab, click the checkerboard swatch under Special, and click Download Photo (300 DPI HD). The tool exports a transparent PNG file ready for ID card templates or editing software.

Why is the first AI cutout slightly slower than subsequent photos?

On your very first photo, your browser downloads the compact segmentation model (~30 MB) and caches it locally. Subsequent photos and future visits process much faster directly from your browser cache.
