Complete Guide to Generating Studio Quality Passport & Visa Photos Online

Creating official government-compliant passport photographs at home has never been easier. The OfficeKit Passport Photo Studio operates 100% locally in your web browser, utilizing state-of-the-art client-side AI body segmentation models. Whether you need standard 35x45 mm photographs for national passports, competitive exams, and government recruitment portals, or 2x2 inch square photographs for US and Schengen visa documentation, our system renders high-density 300 DPI output instantly.

Demonstration of AI background color isolation, 300 DPI crop presets, and multi-up printing sheets.

Traditional online photo editors upload your personal biometric portraits to third-party cloud servers, risking your privacy. In contrast, OfficeKit guarantees zero server interaction. The entire AI background isolation, color correction, skin smoothing, and multi-photo printable sheet creation take place exclusively inside your browser's local sandbox memory.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  • Step 1 - Upload Photo: Click the upload area or drag and drop any clear, front-facing mobile selfie or camera portrait.
  • Step 2 - Background Replacement: Choose from standard government-approved background colors like Royal Blue, Passport Red, or Clean Pure White.
  • Step 3 - Border & Spacing: Toggle the 2px–4px black border option to ensure your photo stands out cleanly when printed on paper.
  • Step 4 - Choose Paper Sheet: Select A4 or 4x6 (4R) paper size to automatically tile dozens of photos across a single page, ready for immediate color printing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

Q: What are the exact dimensions for standard passport and identity photos?
The standard official dimension is 35 mm in width by 45 mm in height (3.5 cm x 4.5 cm). The face should cover 70% to 80% of the vertical area.

Q: Will my photo lose quality or look pixelated?
No. All single-image downloads and complete printable sheets are generated at a sharp 300 DPI print resolution.

Q: Are my photos uploaded or stored anywhere?
No. All calculations run strictly client-side on your device. When you refresh or close the tab, all memory is immediately wiped.
