How to Use the Master Finance Calculator

This tool is designed to handle all your personal and business financial calculations. Here is how to use it:

Step 1: Choose Your Calculator

Use the top tabs to select EMI, Private Loan, SIP, FD, PPF, EPF, NPS, or Compound Interest.

Step 2: Set Your Time Period (Months or Years)

In every tab, you will see a Time Period input. You can type a number (like 6, 18, or 24) and choose Months or Years from the dropdown next to it. The calculator will automatically handle the math.

Step 3: Download CSV Schedule

Click the "Download CSV" button to get a complete breakdown of your payments and interest. The file is Excel-safe and includes the DocEasy.org watermark.

Understanding the Calculators

1. Bank EMI Calculator

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. Whether you are taking a car loan, home loan (mortgage), or a bike loan, this tool uses the standard reducing balance formula to calculate your exact monthly payment.

2. Private Loan Calculator (Monthly Interest)

Local money lenders (sahukars) or private financiers in India often charge interest on a monthly basis (e.g., 10% per month). Use this tab to see exactly how much interest you will pay. You can calculate it for a few months or several years.

3. SIP, FD, PPF, EPF, NPS, and Compound Interest

These investment calculators now support both monthly and yearly timeframes. Whether you want to invest in a 6-month FD, or calculate your PPF for 15 years, you can toggle the dropdown to get the exact result.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate for 18 months?

In the Time Period box, type "18" and select "Months" from the dropdown. The chart and table will automatically show you the breakdown for 1.5 years.

What is the CSV file for?

The CSV file gives you a complete table of your payments or investments. You can open it in Excel or Google Sheets for your personal records.

Does this tool support US Dollars?

Yes. Click the '$ USD' button at the top to convert all calculations and charts into US Dollars.
