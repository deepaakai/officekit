How to Use the All-in-One Finance Calculator

This master tool combines all major financial calculators into one simple page. Here is how to use it:

Step 1: Choose Your Calculator

Use the top tabs to select SIP, FD, PPF, EPF, NPS, Simple Interest, or Compound Interest.

Step 2: Select Currency

Click ₹ for Indian formatting (Lakhs/Crores) or $ for International formatting (Millions).

Step 3: Enter Details

Enter the investment amount, rate, and time. The chart, total values, and year-wise table will update instantly.

Step 4: Download CSV

Click the "Download CSV" button. A file will be downloaded containing the year-wise schedule, complete with the "Generated by DocEasy.org" watermark.

Understanding the Calculators

1. SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

Invest a fixed amount monthly in mutual funds. The chart shows how compounding grows your wealth over time.

2. FD (Fixed Deposit)

A lump sum deposit for a fixed time. Interest is usually compounded quarterly.

3. PPF (Public Provident Fund)

A 15-year government scheme with tax-free returns. You invest once a year, and interest is compounded annually.

4. EPF (Employee Provident Fund)

Your retirement fund. 12% of your basic salary goes into it monthly, matched by your employer. Interest is calculated monthly but compounded annually.

5. NPS (National Pension System)

A retirement scheme where you invest monthly. At age 60, you can withdraw a portion as a lump sum and must buy an annuity with the rest.

6. Simple Interest

Interest calculated only on the principal amount. Best for short-term loans.

7. Compound Interest

Interest calculated on principal + accumulated interest. The frequency (yearly, quarterly, monthly) greatly affects the final amount.
