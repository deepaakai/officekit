How to Use the Age Calculator

Our Age Calculator is very easy to use. You do not need to install any app or create an account. Just follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Enter Your Date of Birth

Click on the first box that says "Date of Birth". A small calendar will open. You can use this calendar to select the exact day, month, and year you were born. If you find it easier, you can also type the date directly using your keyboard.

Step 2: Choose the Calculation Date

The second box says "Calculate age as of". By default, this is set to today's date. This means the calculator will tell you how old you are right now. However, if you want to know how old you will be on a specific future date (like the cutoff date for a government exam), you can change this date.

Step 3: Click "Show My Age"

Once both dates are filled in, click the big blue button. The tool will immediately show your age in a large, easy-to-read format.

Step 4: Read the Results

You will see your exact age in years, months, and days. Below that, you will see how many days, hours, minutes, and seconds you have lived in total. The tool also shows a countdown to your next birthday and the day of the week you were born.

Knowing exactly how many years, months, and days are left until retirement helps people plan their finances better. You can enter your date of birth and set the calculation date to your planned retirement date to see how much time you have.

Have you ever wondered exactly how many days you have lived on this earth? Or how many hours you have spent sleeping? Looking at your age in days and seconds can be a fun and surprising experience.

How Does the Math Work?

The math behind age calculation is not just dividing days by 365. That is because a year has 365 days, but leap years have 366 days. Also, months have different numbers of days (28, 30, or 31).

This calculator uses a standard method. It counts complete years from your birth date. Once it can no longer add a full year, it counts the full months. Finally, it counts the leftover days. This is the exact same method used by banks, government offices, and schools.

For example, if you were born on May 15, 2000, and today is August 20, 2023:

  • From May 15, 2000, to May 15, 2023, is exactly 23 years.
  • From May 15, 2023, to August 15, 2023, is exactly 3 months.
  • From August 15, 2023, to August 20, 2023, is exactly 5 days.

So, your exact age is 23 years, 3 months, and 5 days. Our tool does this math instantly, so you don't have to worry about making mistakes.

Can I use this tool on my mobile phone?

Yes, absolutely. The tool is designed to work perfectly on all devices, including Android phones, iPhones, tablets, and desktop computers.

This is used if you need to know your age on a specific day. For example, if you are applying for a government form where the age limit is calculated as of July 1, 2024, you can select that date to see your exact age on that specific day.
