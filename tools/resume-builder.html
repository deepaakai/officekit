Complete Guide: How to Build an ATS-Friendly Resume in 2026

Finding a job or clearing an interview begins with a standout resume. In 2026, most top companies and recruitment agencies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter thousands of resumes before a human recruiter even looks at them. A poorly formatted resume with complex tables or unreadable elements often gets disqualified immediately. Understanding Different CV & Resume Layouts

Depending on your industry and career level, different layouts offer specific advantages:

  • ATS Classic & Modern Minimal: Ideal for software engineers, finance analysts, and corporate positions where simple typography and structured headings are prioritized.
  • Fresher CV & Indian Declaration Format: Perfectly aligned with Indian government, PSU, and traditional office applications containing detailed parentage, DOB, and declaration clauses.
  • Clinical & Healthcare Professional: Features specialized badging for licenses, BLS certifications, and clinical strengths tailored for nurses and doctors.
  • Executive Sidebar: Uses a prominent left or right column to highlight core proficiencies, software tools, and contact info alongside extensive job experience.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
