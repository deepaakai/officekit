Why 5.5cm × 8.5cm is the Perfect Size

The Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) and most government portals in India require Aadhaar cards to be uploaded in a specific ratio. 5.5cm width by 8.5cm height is the standard portrait size for printed ID cards (CR80/ISO 7810). Using our tool guarantees your document meets these strict requirements for:

  • Indian Government Exams: SSC, UPSC, NEET, JEE, IBPS, RRB, and state-level PSCs require correctly sized ID uploads for admit cards and verification.
  • Local KYC in Delhi NCR: Whether you are in Loni, Ghaziabad, or New Delhi, banks (SBI, HDFC, etc.) and telecom providers require clean Aadhaar/PAN copies.
  • International & NRI Applications: Embassies, visa portals (Schengen, US, UK), and global KYC systems demand standard ID card sizes. If you are an NRI applying for an OCI card or passport renewal, precise cropping is mandatory.
