Complete Guide: Crop Any ID Card at 300 DPI Online

Applying for a government exam, opening a bank account, or verifying your identity on a job portal almost always demands clean, correctly-sized copies of your ID card — both front and back. Whether it's an Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Shram Card, or any other government-issued identity document, the DocEasy ID Card Cropper produces Ultra HD 300 DPI output without ever uploading your sensitive identity documents to any server.

Dual-side crop boxes over a scanned ID card, exporting at exactly 8.5 × 5.5 cm (CR80) in 300 DPI print resolution.

Why 300 DPI Matters

DPI (Dots Per Inch) determines the physical print quality of an image. Government portals, exam authorities, and banks usually specify 200–300 DPI for uploaded ID cards so that text, QR codes, and photos remain legible. This tool renders your cards at 300 DPI by internally using the original PDF's vector data at up to 4× display resolution — guaranteeing razor-sharp output every time.

Single-Side vs Dual-Side Cropping

Some documents contain only one side of a card (e.g., a scanned front only). The tool provides Front and Back toggles in the toolbar:

  • Turn off Back if your document only has the front side. Only the front will be exported and printed.
  • Turn off Front if your document only has the back side. Only the back will be exported and printed.
  • Keep both ON if you have both sides — the tool exports and prints two images (front and back).
  • At least one side must remain enabled at all times.

Password Formats — Common Cases

Many ID card PDFs (especially e-Aadhaar and PAN) are password-protected when downloaded. But the password formats differ:

  • Aadhaar (e-Aadhaar): First 4 letters of your name in CAPITALS + Birth Year.
    Example: Name Deepak, Birth Year 1998 → DEEP1998
  • PAN Card: Your Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format.
    Example: DOB 15 January 1998 → 15011998
  • Any Other Protected PDF: The password your provider gave you. Common patterns are DOB, PAN number, Name + DOB, or PAN + DOB.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  • Step 1 — Upload: Drag-and-drop your ID card PDF, scanned JPG, or phone photo.
  • Step 2 — Enter password (if prompted): Use the correct format for your document.
  • Step 3 — Toggle sides: Turn Front or Back off if your document only has one side.
  • Step 4 — Position crop boxes: Green = Front, Blue = Back. Drag each box to sit over the corresponding card.
  • Step 5 — Adjust size (optional): Default 8.5 × 5.5 cm (CR80 standard).
  • Step 6 — Fine-tune: Use the 8 handles around each box to align edges precisely.
  • Step 7 — Download or print: "Save HD Images" downloads the enabled sides, "Print Ultra HD" opens the print dialog.

How to Move and Resize the Crop Boxes

  • Move: Click anywhere inside the crop box and drag it to the correct position.
  • Resize from corners: Drag the four white square handles at each corner.
  • Resize from edges: Drag the black bar handles in the middle of each side to stretch in one direction only.
  • Mobile support: On phones and tablets, use one finger to drag the box or its handles.

No Photoshop Needed

Because the export renders at true 300 DPI from the original PDF's vector data, there is no need for any external image editor. The tool produces print-ready cards directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my ID card data safe?
Yes. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared.

Q: Can I crop only one side?
Yes. Use the Front and Back toggles in the toolbar to enable or disable each side.

Q: Which ID cards are supported?
Any ID card in the standard CR80 size (8.5 × 5.5 cm) — Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Shram Card, Ration Card, and more.

Q: How do I know my e-Aadhaar password?
First four letters of your name in CAPITALS + year of birth. Example: "Deepak" born 1998 → DEEP1998.

Q: What is my PAN card PDF password?
Usually your Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format. Example: DOB 15/01/1998 → 15011998.

Q: Will the print have a border?
No. The print output is borderless — cards are placed at the top-left corner of an A4 sheet at their true 8.5 × 5.5 cm size.

Q: Can I use this on mobile?
Yes. The crop boxes and handles are fully touch-enabled.

Pro Tips

  • Scan your ID card at 300 DPI in your scanner settings for the sharpest results.
  • Place the card on a matte dark surface in daylight to avoid glare and shadows.
  • After cropping, zoom to 200% to verify the card number and QR code are readable before submitting.
