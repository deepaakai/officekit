Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does converting an image format affect the original quality?
Canvas exports use balanced compression algorithms. PNG outputs retain lossless transparency and clarity, while JPG and WebP files balance high visual fidelity with compact file weights.

Q: How large of a PDF can I unpack into a ZIP?
Because extraction processes sequentially in browser memory, multi-page PDFs with dozens of pages can be extracted easily depending on your device's available RAM.

Q: Is my document data private?
Yes, absolutely. No document, image, or parsed OCR string ever leaves your browser sandbox.
