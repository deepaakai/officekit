FAQ

Q: Does converting an image format affect the original quality?
PNG outputs retain lossless transparency. JPG and WebP balance fidelity with compact weights.

Q: How large of a PDF can I unpack?
Multi-page PDFs with dozens of pages can be extracted depending on device RAM.

Q: Is my document data private?
Yes. No document or extracted text ever leaves your browser sandbox.
