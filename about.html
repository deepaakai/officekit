About DocEasy

Free, private, and fast online tools — built with a privacy-first, browser-based approach.

Our Mission

DocEasy was created to solve a simple but critical problem: the everyday tools we need most — image compression, PDF editing, invoice generation, document scanning — shouldn't require uploading our private files to unknown servers.

Most online tools force you to hand over your documents. Your passports, ID cards, invoices, signatures, and personal photos end up on someone else's server, often without a clear privacy policy. DocEasy takes a completely different approach.

Every tool on DocEasy runs 100% inside your browser. Your files never leave your device. Ever.

What Makes DocEasy Different

Privacy First

Client-side processing means your files stay on your device. Zero uploads, zero storage.

Fast & Reliable

No file uploads means instant processing. Most tools finish in under a second.

Free Forever

No signup, no credit card, no watermark, no hidden limits. All tools are completely free.

No Software Install

Runs entirely in your modern browser. Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS.

What You Can Do With DocEasy

  • Image Tools: Compress images by exact KB, resize for government forms, remove backgrounds from photos and signatures, beautify portraits, convert between formats.
  • PDF Tools: Merge multiple PDFs, split large documents, convert images to PDF, extract pages as JPG, edit PDF text with auto font detection.
  • Identity Tools: Create passport photos (35×45 mm), crop ID cards, resize signatures to exact KB.
  • Business Tools: Generate professional invoices with automatic tax calculation.
  • Productivity Tools: QR code generator, document scanner with OCR, live word and character counter.

Who Built DocEasy

DocEasy is designed and developed by Deepaak Kumar, a developer passionate about building privacy-respecting utilities that solve real everyday problems. Frustrated by tools that required account creation just to compress a single image, the mission became clear: build the simplest, most private, and most useful online toolkit for everyone.
