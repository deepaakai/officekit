About OfficeKit

Free, private, and fast online tools — built with a privacy-first, browser-based approach.

Our Mission

OfficeKit was created to solve a simple but critical problem: the everyday tools we need most — image compression, PDF editing, invoice generation, document scanning — shouldn't require uploading our private files to unknown servers.

Most online tools force you to hand over your documents. Your passports, ID cards, invoices, signatures, and personal photos end up on someone else's server, often without a clear privacy policy. OfficeKit takes a completely different approach.

Every tool on OfficeKit runs 100% inside your browser. Your files never leave your device. Ever.

What Makes OfficeKit Different

Privacy First

Client-side processing means your files stay on your device. Zero uploads, zero storage.

Fast & Reliable

No file uploads means instant processing. Most tools finish in under a second.

Free Forever

No signup, no credit card, no watermark, no hidden limits. All tools are completely free.

No Software Install

Runs entirely in your modern browser. Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS.

What You Can Do With OfficeKit

  • Image Tools: Compress images by exact KB, resize for government forms, remove backgrounds from photos and signatures, beautify portraits, convert between formats.
  • PDF Tools: Merge multiple PDFs, split large documents, convert images to PDF, extract pages as JPG, edit PDF text with auto font detection.
  • Identity Tools: Create passport photos (35×45 mm), crop Aadhaar/PAN cards, resize signatures to exact KB.
  • Business Tools: Generate GST-compliant invoices with automatic CGST/SGST/IGST calculation.
  • Productivity Tools: QR code generator, document scanner with OCR, live word and character counter.

Who Built OfficeKit

OfficeKit is designed and developed by Deepaak Kumar, a developer passionate about building privacy-respecting utilities that solve real everyday problems. Frustrated by tools that required account creation just to compress a single image, the mission became clear: build the simplest, most private, and most useful online toolkit for everyone.

Our Privacy Promise

OfficeKit will never:

  • Upload your files to any server.
  • Store, log, or share your documents.
  • Require account signup to use any tool.
  • Sell or share your personal information.
  • Add watermarks to your output.
  • Limit how many files you can process.
