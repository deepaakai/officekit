Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files really private?

Yes — 100%. OfficeKit runs entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device. No uploads, no server storage, no tracking of file content. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Every tool on OfficeKit is available without signup, email, or payment. Just open the tool and use it.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit. Since processing happens in your browser, the only limit is your device's available memory. Most modern devices handle large files easily.

Will you add watermarks to my output?

Never. All output files are clean, without any watermarks, logos, or branding.

Can I use OfficeKit for commercial work?

Yes. Our tools are free for both personal and professional/commercial use. See our Terms & Conditions for full details.

How do I report a bug or issue?

Email us at contact@pdf365.in with a description of the issue, the tool name, and (if possible) a screenshot. We'll investigate and fix it.

Do you offer API access?

Not currently. OfficeKit is designed as a browser-first set of tools for end-users. If you need bulk automation, feel free to reach out to discuss options.

How can I support OfficeKit?

Share it with friends, colleagues, or on social media — that's the biggest help. You can also email us feedback about what's working and what isn't.

Response Time

We aim to respond to all messages within 24-48 hours (Monday to Friday, India Standard Time). For urgent bug reports, please include "URGENT" in the subject line.

For legal matters, please also review our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
