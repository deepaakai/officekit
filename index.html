Why DocEasy is Different

Most "free online tools" upload your files to a server. DocEasy runs everything locally in your browser using Canvas API, PDF-lib, WebAssembly, and MediaRecorder. Your files never leave your device. Verify it yourself: open Developer Tools (F12), watch the Network tab, and you'll see zero outgoing requests.
