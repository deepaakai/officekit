FAQ

We answer your questions

Everything you need to know about using DocEasy, anywhere in the world.

Is DocEasy really free?

Yes — every tool is completely free with no signup, no watermarks, and no hidden limits. Nothing to install either.

Are my files uploaded to your servers?

No. DocEasy uses modern browser APIs to process files entirely on your device. Your documents never leave your computer or phone.

Do I need an account?

Never. Every tool works without a login, email, or payment. Just open a tool and start using it right away.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), Excel (XLS/XLSX), and PowerPoint (PPT/PPTX). Convert, compress, edit, and merge them all in your browser.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. DocEasy works on any device with a modern browser — desktop, tablet, or phone, anywhere in the world.

How large can my files be?

Most tools accept files up to 100 MB. Processing happens in your browser, so the real limit is your device's available memory.
