What is DocEasy?

DocEasy is a free, privacy-first web platform offering more than 50 online tools that run entirely inside your web browser. Unlike traditional online tools that upload your files to a remote server, DocEasy processes everything locally on your device using modern web technologies like Canvas API, PDF-lib, WebAssembly, and MediaRecorder API. Your files never leave your device.

The platform is designed for students, job seekers, cyber cafe operators, CSC center operators, freelancers, small business owners, teachers, and anyone who needs to handle document, image, or media tasks quickly without installing software or creating an account.

Why DocEasy is Different

Most "free online tools" websites work the same way: you upload your file to their server, they process it, and you download the result. The problem? Your private documents — Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, bank statements, salary slips, family photos — sit in some stranger's cloud storage, often for days, sometimes forever.

DocEasy flips this model on its head. Every tool runs entirely inside your browser using modern web technologies:

  • Canvas API — for image and video processing
  • PDF-lib — for reading and writing PDFs
  • WebAssembly — for running heavy libraries like FFmpeg and OpenCV at native speed
  • MediaRecorder API — for screen, webcam, and voice recording
  • Web Speech API — for text-to-speech
  • AI models in-browser — for background removal and card detection

The result: your files never leave your device. You can verify this yourself — open Developer Tools (F12), switch to the Network tab, upload a file, and watch. You'll see zero requests carrying your data to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DocEasy really free?

Yes. Every tool on DocEasy is completely free with no signup, no watermarks, and no hidden limits. You can use unlimited tools without paying anything.

Are my files uploaded to your servers?

No. All tools process files entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript, Canvas API, WebAssembly, and PDF libraries. Your documents never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Do I need an account?

Never. Every tool works without a login, email verification, phone number, or payment. Just open the page and start using it.

Which file formats are supported?

DocEasy supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), Excel (XLS/XLSX), and PowerPoint (PPT/PPTX). Output formats include PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX, WAV, WebM, and more depending on the tool.

Can I use DocEasy on mobile?

Yes. Every tool is fully responsive and works on Android and iOS browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and Samsung Internet. Desktop, tablet, and phone are all supported.

Can I merge PDF, Word, Excel, and PPT files?

Yes. The Universal Merger combines PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files in one tool. For PPT files, we recommend a two-step workflow (PPT to PDF, then Merge PDF) for best quality.

Can I password-protect a PDF?

Yes. Use the PDF Locker & Unlocker tool. You can set user and owner passwords, and choose what actions you allow (printing, copying, modifying, form filling).

Does DocEasy work offline?

After a tool's page loads once, many tools continue to work offline since all processing happens in your browser. This is especially useful for cyber cafes and CSC centers with unreliable internet.

Are there watermarks on output files?

No. DocEasy does not add watermarks to your output files. Some tools offer an optional watermark toggle, but it is off by default.

Which tool should I use to merge Aadhaar or PAN front and back?

Use the 2 in 1 ID Maker tool. It auto-removes the background, auto-crops the card, and merges both sides onto a single A4 page in actual size (85.6 × 54 mm) — perfect for bank, government, and exam applications.

Is the Resume Builder really ATS-friendly?

Yes. The DocEasy Resume Builder includes 30 recruiter-tested templates with clean section headings, standard chronological layout, and no broken layers. All templates pass through Applicant Tracking Systems used by major companies.

Do I need to pay to download my resume?

No. You can build, edit, save drafts, and download your resume as a print-ready PDF completely free. No payment, no signup, no watermark.

Which tools support AI processing?

The Image Background Remover and the 2 in 1 ID Maker use in-browser AI models to automatically remove backgrounds. Everything runs locally on your device, so nothing is uploaded to any server.

Can I use DocEasy for commercial purposes?

Yes. Cyber cafes, CSC centers, print shops, freelancers, and small businesses can use all DocEasy tools freely for commercial work. There is no license fee or usage restriction.

Does DocEasy work on old computers or slow phones?

Most tools work on any modern browser, even on older devices. Heavier tools that use AI models may be slower on very old phones. For best performance, use Chrome or Edge on desktop.
