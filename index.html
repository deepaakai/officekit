What you can do on DocEasy

DocEasy is for everyday document work: merge or compress a PDF for a job portal, convert DOCX to PDF, resize a photo to 20KB or 50KB, fix PAN card photo and signature size, put Aadhaar front and back on one A4 page, make passport photos, build a resume, or work out EMI, GST and income tax.

Tools run in your browser. After a page loads, most tools keep working even if your connection is slow.

Frequently asked questions

Is DocEasy free?

Yes. The tools are free to use with no signup. We do not add watermarks to output files.

Are my files uploaded?

Files are processed in your browser on your device. Use the Network tab in browser developer tools to check.

How do I resize a PAN card photo and signature?

Open the PAN Photo & Signature tool, upload your photo and signature, and download the resized files in the required pixel size.

How do I put Aadhaar front and back on one page?

Use the 2 in 1 ID Maker. It crops both sides and places them on one A4 page at actual card size.

How do I convert DOCX to PDF?

Open Word to PDF, choose your DOC or DOCX file and download the PDF.

Can I use DocEasy at a cyber cafe or CSC?

Yes. Cyber cafes, CSC centres and small businesses can use the tools for daily work.
