1. Loan Mathematics & EMI Amortization

Borrowing capital for assets like residential property, vehicles, or higher education creates multi-decade liabilities where the cost of borrowed money often outpaces the initial principal sum. The Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) represents a mathematically smoothed repayment stream comprising both principal repayment and accrued interest components across each calendar cycle.

Every commercial bank in India computes monthly liabilities using the universal standard annuity formulation: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1). Where P represents the principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the tenure in months.

2. Salary Architecture & CTC Deconstruction

Cost to Company (CTC) is an aggregate metric that includes direct cash components, indirect social security contributions, statutory retiral reserves, and performance-linked variables. Basic salary forms the foundation of your compensation package (typically 40% to 50% of gross CTC), scaling PF and gratuity allocations directly.

3. Retirement Security: Gratuity, EPF, & NPS

For organizations with 10 or more employees, gratuity is a statutory entitlement triggered after 5 years of continuous service. It is computed as: (15 × Last Drawn Basic Salary × Years) ÷ 26. Combined with 12% mandatory provident fund deposits, these form the primary safety net for private-sector retirement.
